### T66: under a tight register target ptxas recomputes the CTA shared window base (S2UR SR_CgaCtaId, UMOV, ULEA) in front of every shared atomic or store reached through a generic pointer, pin the 32-bit shared address through an empty volatile asm
applies to: every primitive that holds its temp storage or output by generic reference and issues shared atomics or stores from an unrolled item loop (B1 to B6, N6, N9, W3 and W5 finishes), sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92
measured: B1_det3 at 4497544a4f (generic atomicAdd_block on storage.selected_offset[item_class]) against B1_det3 at e1c6ed6816 (the same atomic as atom.shared.add on a 32-bit address taken once with __cvta_generic_to_shared and pinned with asm volatile("" : "+r"(addr))), each against B1_det2 in its own lock section on umbriel-b200-018, 2026-10-25, harness 0.8 (batches state/results/opt-B1det3/B1_det3_vs_B1_det2_focus_d3a_20261025_173718.json and _d4a_20261025_175424.json). Latency against the same control: bf16 N 4096 k 16, 64, 256 plus 2.2, 2.4, 4.8 percent before against minus 1.6, 4.1, 4.7 after (4890 against 4579 cycles at k 64, det2 4775 and 4776), fp32 N 4096 k 16, 64, 256 plus 4.4, 6.6, 8.3 against plus 1.4, minus 0.1, minus 1.3 (4945 against 4632 at k 64, no tie phase runs on fp32 rows), fp32 N 16384 k 64 and 256 plus 1.3 and 5.7 against minus 2.7 and 2.3 (14482 against 13365 at k 256), bf16 N 16384 k 256 minus 8.2 against minus 14.9. About 300 to 1100 cycles per call at 16 to 64 item slots. Throughput unchanged within 2 percent, the extra instructions hide behind 5 to 8 warps per scheduler. The one-block phase profile shows the same in the epilogue (1240 against 1087 cycles at bf16 N 4096 k 64, 4683 against 4030 at N 16384 k 256, state/results/opt-B1det3/prof/).
evidence: cuobjdump of the harness latency kernels at fp32 N 4096 k 64, 37 S2UR SR_CgaCtaId in the det3 kernel against 2 in det2's in the same binary, every ticket site reading `S2UR UR10, SR_CgaCtaId / UMOV UR9, 0x400 / ULEA UR9, UR10, UR9, 0x18 / UIADD3 UR10, UR9, 0x4 / IMAD.U32 R2, UR10 / @!P3 IMAD R2, UR9 / ATOMS.ADD R7, [R2+0x84c], R7` where det2 keeps the base in UR8 across the epilogue (`UIADD3 UR13, UR8, 0x4 / IMAD.U32 R15, UR13 / @!P4 IMAD R15, UR8 / ATOMS.ADD`). det3 at 48 registers (20 to 72 spill bytes outside the timed chain), det2 at 64. With the conversion taken once but not pinned: still 37, the conversion itself is rematerialized. Pinned: 6. Files state/results/opt-B1det3/B1_det3__B1_det2__fp32_T256_N4096_K64_prefer_smaller_index.sass.gz (before) and .d4.sass.gz (conversion taken once, not pinned).
how to use: When ptxas lands a kernel on an occupancy register target (48, 64, 128) and the SASS shows S2UR SR_CgaCtaId per store or atomic site, convert the shared pointer once (`__cvta_generic_to_shared`), keep the 32-bit result in a register the compiler cannot recompute (`asm volatile("" : "+r"(addr))`), and issue the accesses on that address plus an offset (`atom.shared.add.u32`, `st.shared.b32`, or plain C++ on a pointer rebuilt from it). Check with `grep -c SR_CgaCtaId` per kernel: a handful is normal, one per site is the pitfall. Check the control too: an A/B between two variants that land on different register targets measures the pitfall and not the variant, det2 at 64 registers did not have it.
caveats: One toolkit (nvcc 13.4.92, x86 host). Seen in the harness latency kernel, which inlines two copies of the primitive, where the register target binds, and in the one-block profiler kernel, not in the throughput kernel of the same TU at the same cell. The pin keeps one register live across the epilogue and changed neither the register count nor the spill bytes. The ticket atomic spelled in PTX stays a plain ATOMS (no T23 aggregation), and asm volatile keeps the sites in program order, which they were anyway.
