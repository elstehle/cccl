### T?: a fixed-point image of the key over the tile's value bracket gives radix select a first digit with 256 equal-width value bins, one FADD, one FFMA and two IMNMX per item, and exact exits by count or by tracked tie class
applies to: radix and bucket select at block scope (B1, B3, B4 finishing stages) and warp scope (W3, N1 digits), fp32 and bf16 keys, any k with a valid lower bound on the k-th largest (k at most Threads with the minimum over threads of the per-thread maximum, else the tile minimum)
measured: N6 prototype against B1_idx (the same pass machinery on radix digits), 256 threads, B200 umbriel-b200-068, CUDA 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness latency chain slope, file state/results/opt-B1/B1_n6_vs_B1_idx_focus_n6b_20261025_131315.json (card B1_n6_b200_20261025_1): fp32 N 4096 k 16 2887 against 3663 cycles, k 64 3906 against 4585, k 256 4607 against 5047, bf16 N 4096 k 16 2846 against 3369, N 1024 k 16 bf16 1700 against 1768 and fp32 1673 against 1794, with the build's register handicap (T on the per-pass recompute). Host simulation approaches/B1/sim_passes.md: on random normal input the pivot bin after one 256-bin pass over the thread-max bracket holds 1.1 to 7.3 items on average and at most 16 for every k at most 256 at N 1024 to 32768, against 56 to 139 candidates after the first radix pass, and the fp32 8-bit radix schedule needs 3 passes in 24 to 89 percent of the rows while the fixed-point schedule is exact after 2 in 97 to 100 percent.
evidence: approaches/B1/block_topk_n6.cuh (fixed_point, run_u_pass), per item per pass FADD, FFMA with the 2^23 rounding constant, IMNMX, IMNMX, LOP3, ISETP, SHF, LOP3, ATOMS against LOP3, ISETP, SHF, LOP3, ATOMS for the radix digit (cuobjdump of the n6b and idx1 binaries).
how to use: Reduce the tile to hi (max) and lo (min over threads of the per-thread max for k at most Threads, else the min) with redux.sync (T1) and one barrier that the histogram zeroing needs anyway, set scale = (2^23 - 8) / (hi - lo) finite and nonzero in every case, and take the digits of u = clamp(bits(fmaf(v - lo, scale, 2^23)), bits(2^23), bits(2^23) + 2^23 - 1) instead of the twiddled key's: 8, 8 and 7 bits. The map is monotone (every operation rounds monotonically) so items in higher buckets are larger, the early exit (pivot count equals remaining k) is exact as in radix select, and candidates that still share a u after its 23 bits are resolved by radix passes on the key bits restricted to them, starting at the byte that holds the highest differing bit of their tracked min and max. Track the candidates' key min and max per bucket from the second pass on (two fire-and-forget atomics per candidate) and exit when the pivot bucket is one tie class. Fall back to key passes when the first pass leaves more than a quarter of the tile as candidates (heavy tail).
caveats: Values below lo collapse into the lowest bucket (correct, resolved by the fallback if the pivot lands there). -0.0 and +0.0 share an image. The per-item cost is about 4 instructions per pass above the radix digit, which is 7 percent of the histogram sweep at IPT 128 and hidden in the dependency-bound pass at IPT 4. Tracking over all candidates instead of per bucket detects a bf16 tie class one pass late, and the compiler must be kept from hoisting the image across passes (previous entry), both corrected in the n6b variant that is measured next.
