### T?: a per-pass recompute of a loop-invariant per-item key image is hoisted by the compiler into IPT live registers, pin it with an empty asm on the invariant inputs
applies to: every multi-pass selection that derives a per-item digit from the key and block-uniform parameters (N6 fixed-point image, B3 and B4 threshold tests, W3 digit extraction with a runtime shift), all scopes, large items per thread above all
measured: N6 fixed-point bucket select, 256 threads, B200 umbriel-b200-068, CUDA 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness latency chain slope and ptxas spill counts, files state/results/opt-B1/B1_n6_vs_B1_idx_focus_n6a_20261025_130910.json and B1_n6_vs_B1_idx_focus_n6b_20261025_131315.json (card B1_n6_b200_20261025_1). Two builds of the same algorithm that differ by one IMNMX per item and the scale expression: 40 registers at IPT 4 in both, 64 against 80 at IPT 16, 1464 to 1508 against 8572 to 8588 bytes of spill at IPT 128, bf16 N 32768 k 16 11983 against 30398 cycles (2.5 times), fp32 N 4096 k 16 2539 against 2887. The radix control B1_idx in the same sessions: 32 registers at IPT 4, 47 to 48 at IPT 16, 250 with 0 spill at IPT 128.
evidence: ptxas -v and cuobjdump of the two builds in the gen directories of the batches, the image computation (FADD, FFMA, IMNMX) appears once per item before the first pass in the spilling build and the passes reload it, in the other build it is recomputed per pass. The next build pins the recompute with `asm volatile("" : "+f"(lo), "+f"(scale))` per pass (approaches/B1/block_topk_n6.cuh, opaque()).
how to use: When a pass loop recomputes a cheap function of a per-item register and block-uniform parameters, the compiler may CSE it across passes and keep one result per item live across the whole pass sequence, which doubles the register footprint of the keys. Pass the uniform parameters through an empty asm statement at the start of every pass so each pass sees fresh inputs, at zero instruction cost, and compare the ptxas register and spill counts of the two spellings. The AIR radix passes do not have this problem because their digit is a shift and mask of the key register itself.
caveats: The asm barrier also blocks legitimate hoisting of the parameters' own arithmetic (one FMUL of the scale), negligible. Whether the compiler hoists depends on small changes elsewhere in the kernel (the two builds above differ by one instruction per item), so the pin is insurance, not a measured gain by itself until the n6c batch lands.
