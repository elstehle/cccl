### T?: nvcc 13.4 warp-aggregates a shared atomicAdd with a provably uniform address and constant increment, inserting S2R SR_LANEID and S2R SR_LTMASK per site, about 80 cycles each on the critical path
applies to: every ticket, counter or histogram atomic in a per-item loop, all scopes, all approaches on sm_100 (B1 to B6, N1, N6, W3, W5)
measured: block_topk_air radix select with an index-only epilogue, deterministic variant, 256 threads, IPT 4, fp32 keys without ties (the tie phase never runs), B200 umbriel-b200-068, CUDA 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness latency chain slope, file state/results/opt-B1/B1_det_vs_B1_idx_focus_det1_20261025_131043.json (card B1_det_b200_20261025_1). Epilogue with `atomicAdd_block(&counter[0], 1)` (uniform address): 2138 cycles at N 1024 k 16, 2268 at k 64, 4503 at N 4096 k 16. The same epilogue with `atomicAdd_block(&counter[item_class], 1)` (data-dependent index): 1794, 1902 and 3666. About 86 cycles per item at IPT 4 and 52 per item at IPT 16 for 4 and 16 aggregated sites.
evidence: cuobjdump -sass of the latency kernel: per item `S2R R9, SR_LANEID`, `VOTEU.ANY UR12, UPT, PT`, `FLO.U32 R18, UR12`, `S2R R19, SR_LTMASK`, `POPC R16, UR12`, `ISETP.EQ.U32.AND P1, PT, R18, R9`, `LOP3.LUT R19, R19, UR12`, `POPC R19, R19`, `@P1 ATOMS.ADD R17, [UR8+0x84c], R16`, `SHFL.IDX PT, R12, R17, R18, 0x1f`, `IMAD.IADD R12, R12, 0x1, R19`, `STS [R12+0x8e0], R11` (approaches/B1/REPORT.md section 4 quotes the region). The kernel with the data-dependent index has no S2R between the two CS2R SR_CLOCKLO of the timed region, the aggregated one has 8. T14 measured the S2R shadow at about 80 cycles.
how to use: Check the SASS of every per-item atomic site for S2R SR_LTMASK. When the compiler aggregated and the site is on a dependent path (the returned ticket feeds a store), make the address or the increment opaque: index the counter with a value the compiler cannot prove uniform (the item's class, or `int c = 0; asm volatile("" : "+r"(c));`), which restores the plain per-item ATOMS. Aggregation is right only when the sites are off the critical path and the same-address contention is the bottleneck (T9, 256-way bursts).
caveats: One compiler version (nvcc 13.4.92, cicc x86). The aggregation heuristic may differ on other versions and for global atomics. The fix with the opaque index is implemented in approaches/B1/block_topk_b1.cuh and not yet measured (batch budget), the figures above compare the two epilogue spellings that were measured.
