### T?: inverting a bucket image to a value threshold: validate the edge with the image itself and step it by the magnitude of the base, not by ulps of the edge
applies to: every equi-width bucket or fixed-point image that turns a pivot bin back into a value threshold (N3's running threshold, N6 and B4 brackets, GVR style threshold bins, any W5 style threshold derived from a bin edge), fp32 and bf16 keys
measured: host model of N3's exact device arithmetic (approaches/N3/model.py, numpy float32 with a single-rounding fma), N 32768 k 2048 fed as chunks of 4096, 1024 bins over the first chunk's bracket [-3.66, 3.65], 2026-10-25. With the edge stepped by nextafterf four times, the validation failed at bin 644 (edge 0.651, the image of the stepped edge stayed 644), the threshold fell back to the bracket minimum for chunks 1 and 2 and the buffer took 12384 entries instead of 11148, with a threshold of -0.033 where the exact k-th largest was 0.655. With a margin of 2.4e-7 times the larger magnitude of edge and base, quadrupled per retry, the validated edge is found at the first or second try in every cell. GPU confirmation of the fixed build: 24 cells x 22 oracle cases passed (B200 umbriel-b200-068, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, state/results/opt-N3/N3_stream_vs_N8_b3tuned_n3_a_oracle_20261025_170906.json), the unfixed build never ran on a GPU.
evidence: approaches/N3/block_topk_stream.cuh `lower_edge` and approaches/N3/model.py `lower_edge`. The image is bin = round((v - lo) x scale) computed as fmaf(v - lo, scale, 2^23): the subtraction rounds to the ulp of the difference (4.8e-7 at a difference of 4.3), which is 8 ulps of the edge (6e-8 at 0.65), so a one-ulp step of the edge leaves the difference, and the bin, unchanged.
how to use: Compute the candidate edge t = lo + (p - 0.5) / scale, require image(t) < p with the same device arithmetic the sweep uses (then every key in bins at or above p is strictly above t by monotonicity), and when the test fails step t down by eps = max(|t|, |lo|) x 2^-22, quadrupling eps per retry, with the bracket minimum (always a valid lower bound) as the fallback after a handful of retries. Never step by nextafterf of the edge alone. The same holds for any threshold derived from an image whose first operation subtracts or scales the key.
caveats: A relative margin admits a few extra candidates per chunk (the keys between the stepped edge and the true edge), negligible against a chunk of 4096. Guard the degenerate scales first (zero or infinite, T60): the division yields inf or NaN and the fallback applies. One arithmetic form measured (FADD then FFMA, T25's), a form that folds lo into the fma constant has the same problem with a different ulp.
