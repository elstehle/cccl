### T?: a rolled loop over a shared-memory buffer costs 180 to 320 cycles per iteration, and one data-dependent shared atomic per register slot costs about 100 cycles per slot in an unrolled sweep
applies to: every finish or compaction stage that sweeps a shared-memory candidate buffer (B3's compacted passes, B4's candidate loads, N3's finish), every histogram or bucket pass with one shared atomic per item slot (B1, B3 and N6 first passes, N3's store sweep), block scope at 256 and 512 threads
measured: N3 phase profile (approaches/N3/profile_phases.cu, clock64 stamps by thread 0, median of 32 calls, one block, random normal input, B200 umbriel-b200-068, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, file approaches/N3/profiles/profile_random_b200_068.txt). Finish sweeps over 11.1k to 11.3k buffered entries written as `#pragma unroll 1` loops (LDS.64, FADD, FFMA, two IMNMX, two compares, then counts or predicated STS per iteration): 8079 and 11878 cycles for about 44 iterations per thread at 256 threads (184 and 270 cycles per iteration), 4601 and 7026 for 22 iterations at 512 threads (209 and 319). Store sweep over 16 register slots per thread (one predicated STS.64, a 5-instruction fixed-point image, one branch-free ATOMS.ADD into one of 1024 bins or a per-thread dummy word, one IADD): 1631 to 1721 cycles at 256 threads, about 100 cycles per slot, and 1100 to 1308 for 8 slots at 512 threads, about 140 per slot. Contrast from the B4 report on the same node with the same method: B4_v3's compaction store sweep without atomics and image about 15 cycles per slot, its unrolled candidate passes about 10 cycles per slot, a cub BlockScan 350 to 430 cycles. The whole call: 43195 cycles in the harness at 256 threads fp32 N 32768 k 2048 against B3_tuned 20775 (card N3_stream_b200_20261025_1).
evidence: the profile file above and the SASS statistics of the harness latency kernel (24120 instructions at fp32 N 32768, run.py static analysis in state/results/opt-N3/N3_stream_vs_B3_tuned_n3_c_20261025_171324.json). The rolled loop issues one iteration's dependent chain at a time (LDS latency about 30 cycles, then 6 to 8 dependent ALU instructions, then a predicated store), and the next iteration's load does not start before the loop-carried counters of the current one resolve.
how to use: Never leave a sweep over a shared buffer rolled when the entry count per thread is in the tens: unroll by 8, load the 8 entries first, then classify and store, so 8 independent chains overlap (in a finish the register tile is dead and the registers are free). Budget one data-dependent shared atomic per register slot at about 100 cycles per slot at two warps per scheduler, ten times a compare-and-store slot, and cut the number of atomics rather than the branch around them: histogram the compacted candidates instead of every slot (N3 issues 128 atomics per thread where 45 would do), or per-warp histograms when inter-warp contention is the cost (unmeasured, open question in the N3 card).
caveats: One node, one toolkit, random input. The 100 cycles per slot bundle the atomic with the 5-instruction image and the predicated 8-byte store; the B4 contrast (15 cycles per slot without them) attributes the difference to the atomic and the image together, a same-kernel A/B that drops the atomic was not run. Static BAR.SYNC and instruction counts include inlined fallback paths that never execute (237 barriers in the N3 kernel, about 30 execute).
