### T?: a per-item shared atomic into a per-warp histogram beats five pipelined ballots per item by 1.2x to 1.5x at warp scope, data-dependent addresses included
applies to: warp-scope digit and bucket histograms (N6 at warp scope, W3 radix passes, N1 at wider digits), 16 to 256 bins, items per lane 8 to 32, any key width once the bucket index is an integer
measured: N6w_atom32 against N6w_v2 (identical algorithm and finish, only the 32-bin bracket histogram differs: one fire-and-forget atomicAdd per item into a per-warp 32-word shared histogram against five ballots per item with the per-lane bucket match by LOP3 and popc), same lock section, GB200 gb200-nvl4-ts2-105 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness latency chain slope and one-wave throughput, random normal input, card cards/N6_w_atom32_gb200_20261025_1.json, batch state/results/opt-N6w/N6w_atom32_vs_N6w_v2_n6w_atom32b_20261025_162114.json. bf16 N 256 k 16 1096 against 1410 cycles, N 512 k 16 1644 against 2184, N 512 k 64 1879 against 2429, N 512 k 256 2564 against 3116, N 1024 k 16 3000 against 4631, N 1024 k 64 3682 against 5493, N 1024 k 256 4740 against 7154 (1.18x to 1.54x), fp32 within 5 percent of the same ratios, throughput 507 against 349 Gelem per s at N 512 k 64 and 485 against 322 at N 1024 k 16 (1.3x to 1.9x). At IPT 64 and 128 both variants sit at 255 registers (8 bytes and 4 KB of spill) and the gain shrinks to 1.1x or reverses by up to 20 percent.
evidence: harness/adapters/N6w_atomic_impl.cuh (bracket_pass_atomic) against harness/adapters/N6w_impl.cuh (bracket_pass). SASS of the ballot variant's N 512 k 64 bf16 latency kernel: 678 VOTE, 1656 LOP3, 262 POPC in 6160 instructions, VOTE interleaved every five to six instructions (the ballots already pipeline at the T17 rate), so the saving is the 24 instructions per item of predicates, bucket match and popc plus the 30 cycles of VOTE throughput, not a latency effect. The atomic variant issues an IMAD for the address and one ATOMS per item, zeroes the histogram with one store per lane, and reads one word per lane after a __syncwarp.
how to use: Count a warp-local histogram with one atomicAdd per item into shared memory words owned by the warp, one bucket per word, and read the counts back after a __syncwarp. Keep the atomic unconditional (items that must not count add to a per-lane dummy word, T32) and let the address depend on the data, which is what prevents the ptxas aggregation of T26 and T27 and is harmless here: 32 lanes on 32 random words replay about 3 to 4 times and still cost far less than five ballots. The ballot form keeps its place only where no shared memory is available or where a single ballot per item suffices (one key bit, N1). Budget the pass at about 10 instructions per item plus the scan.
caveats: One toolkit and SM (nvcc 13.4.92, sm_100 on GB200), one warp per block in the latency regime and eight warps per SM in the throughput regime, both faster. Measured with the bin image computed per item (FADD, FFMA, two IMNMX), a radix digit is cheaper still. Shared memory per warp: 128 bytes for 32 bins plus 128 bytes of dummy words. The gain vanishes once the kernel is register bound (IPT 64 and above in these builds). Contended replays were not isolated, the measurement is the whole pass.
