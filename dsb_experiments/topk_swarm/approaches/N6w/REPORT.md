## 1. What it is

One state serves all three approaches: a warp-uniform range [tmin, tmax] of order-preserving key images (the twiddled float, bf16 keys keep their 16 bits in the high half and zero below), the number n of candidates inside it and the number r of them still needed. A pass refines the range and subtracts the candidates above the new range from r. Items strictly above the range are selected, items below are dropped, both without per-item state: the final emission compares every item's image against a threshold and writes the selected items at their rank among the selected items in index order (one ballot per item slot, lane-major rank), so `prefer_smaller_index` costs nothing beyond the tie-rank ballots that only run when a tie class is cut. The oracle battery passes on every measured cell of every adapter, both requirements measure identical cycles.

- N6 warp (`N6w_ballot`, `N6w_v2`, `N6w_v3`): bracket [lo, hi] with hi the warp maximum and lo the minimum over lanes of the lane's m-th largest key, m = ceil(k / 32) rounded up to a power of two, exact for k at most 256 (32 m items are at or above lo), the full range above. One pass of 32 equal width bins over the bracket: per item one FADD, one FFMA with the 2^23 constant and two IMNMX give the bin in the low 5 bits of the image, five ballots per item give the per-bit lane masks, lane j combines them into the mask of its bucket and counts with popc, a 5-step shuffle suffix scan locates the pivot bin, two redux give the exact image range of the pivot bin. Finish: all candidates when n equals r, index rank when the pivot bin is one tie class, else the packed W2 rounds on at most 64 (IPT at most 16) or 128 compacted candidates ((image, inverted index) words in shared memory, r redux.max rounds over 2 or 4 words per lane), else another bracket pass over the candidates' exact value range (at most four), then radix digit passes as the last resort.
- `N6w_atom32`, `N6w_atom32f`, `N6w_atom128`: the same with the histogram counted by one fire-and-forget shared atomic per item into a per-warp histogram of 32 or 128 words (data-dependent addresses, items outside the range add to a per-lane dummy word so the atomic stays unconditional, T32).
- W3 (`W3_d4c`, `W3_d5c`, `W3_d4`): digit passes over the key image from the top with 4 or 5 bit digits, one ballot per digit bit plus one of the candidate flag, lane j counts bucket j, the same suffix scan, early exit when the pivot bucket holds exactly r. With compaction (`c` variants) the candidates move to shared memory in index order once they fit into N/4 slots and the remaining passes run over IPT/4 slots per lane.
- N1 (`N1_bitserial`): one pass per key bit, one ballot per item of (candidate and bit set), the popcounts summed, the count against r decides the bit, no scan, no shared memory.

The host simulation (`sim_warp_bins.md`, 200 rows per cell) predicted and the GPU confirmed the decisive statistic: with the exact bound the pivot bin after one 32-bin pass holds 8 to 11 items at k 64, 12 to 21 at k 128, 24 to 39 at k 256 (max 61) at N 256 to 4096, so the packed W2 finish runs on at most 64 compacted candidates in 85 to 97 percent of the rows with about n / 2 rounds and the rest exit whole. k 1024 with the full bracket leaves 175 to 300 items and needs the second bracket pass, which leaves 6 to 11.

## 2. Contract

Input blocked in registers (lane t holds items t IPT to t IPT + IPT - 1), N a multiple of 32 up to 32768 (the scan packs two 16-bit counts into one shuffle word, W3 with compaction needs N at least 128), k in [1, N/2], keys bf16 or fp32 (widened to fp32 in registers, minus zero folded onto plus zero so both zeros are one tie class as the oracle requires), int32 indices derived from the position. Output k indices in the harness's shared array, unsorted (index order for N6's exit paths, W3 and N1, items above the pivot bin in index order then the finish's descending key order for N6's W2 path). Temp storage: N6w ballot variants 256 or 512 bytes per warp (the finish compaction buffer), the atomic variants 256 or 640 bytes more (histogram and dummy words), W3 with compaction N/2 words per warp (8 KB at N 4096), W3_d4 and N1 none. Synchronization: ballots, redux.sync, shuffles and __syncwarp over the full mask, shared atomics in the atomic variants, no barriers, the caller separates calls that reuse the temp storage or the output by a __syncwarp. NaN unsupported (its image is unordered), infinities and denormals are ordinary keys (the bracket guard of T60). Both requirements run the same code.

The first batch measured about 75 instructions per item on the common path (pass 28, post-scan 10, finish 35) and the SASS showed ptxas already interleaving the ballots across items (VOTE every five to six instructions in a 6160-instruction kernel with 678 VOTE, 1656 LOP3, 262 POPC), so the pass was issue bound at the ballot throughput floor (five ballots at about 6 to 7 cycles each per T17 and T33). Replacing the five ballots by one shared atomic took 1.2x to 1.5x off every cell up to IPT 32 and 1.3x to 1.9x in throughput. Below IPT 64 what remains is dominated by dependent fixed costs rather than per-item issue: two redux and a float division for the bracket, the 5-step shuffle scan (about 190 cycles), the pivot ballot, popc and shuffle (about 80), the serial per-item min and max chain of the pivot bin's images (about 10 cycles per item), two __syncwarp with their LDS and about n / 2 redux rounds. Trimming 13 instructions per item from the finish changed nothing at IPT 8 to 32 (card N6_w_atom32f), which is the evidence.

Registers: 64 at IPT 8, 80 to 116 at 16, 128 to 168 at 32, 255 at IPT 64 (8 to 28 bytes of spill) and 255 with a 4 to 5 KB stack frame, 600 to 800 bytes of spill, about 400 STL and 700 LDL at IPT 128 (N 4096), where every variant runs 60 to 80 thousand cycles against W2's 6.5 to 48 thousand: a local-memory artifact of the float tile plus the per-item bins plus the finish arrays (T36), not the algorithm. At IPT 64 the schedule lottery of a full register file decides between variants (the trimmed finish is 1.2x to 1.5x faster for fp32 and 5 to 9 percent slower for bf16 at N 2048 on the same instruction stream, T28 and T50 style).

## 5. Ideas not yet tried

- The dependent fixed costs that now dominate below IPT 64: a ballot scan over the count bits instead of the 5-step shuffle scan (about 70 cycles), an approximate reciprocal for the bracket scale (any positive scale keeps the map monotone), tree-reduced per-item min and max accumulators for the pivot bin (about 100 cycles at IPT 16), and a redux-based pivot search.
- The register footprint at IPT 64 and 128: a two-half item loop over the tile, or recomputing the image from a packed 16-bit key for bf16 (half the tile registers), which decides whether N6w can own N 2048 to 4096 at all and whether the fp32 cells at N 2048 stop being a schedule lottery.
- Candidate compaction before N6's second bracket pass (k 1024: at most N/8 candidates remain, the pass still sweeps the whole tile with six ballots per item), the W3 compaction code applies as is once the bracket pass takes a compacted (image, index) array.
- N6w_atom128 (built, not measured): four times smaller pivot bins, more whole-bin exits, fewer W2 rounds, at the cost of a 4-bin-per-lane scan.
- W3 with the atomic histogram (one atomic per item per pass) and 8-bit digits, W3_d5c and W3_d4 (both built, not measured within the eight batches).
- The W2 finish list depth as a function of IPT (2 words per lane up to IPT 16, 4 above) was set from the cost model and not swept, the rounds' cost was not isolated (the k slope is carried by the per-lane top-m bound).
- An `sm_100f` variant has no lever: the bracket uses two u32 redux on images per call, T2 saves about 18 cycles per redux round.
- A sub-warp form of N1 (ballots serve every logical warp at once, T37 and T40) for its own cells at LW 8 N 128 k 64.

## 6. Determinism and ordering support

Set deterministic by construction (exact selection). `prefer_smaller_index` is inherent in every path: the W2 finish orders by (key, inverted index), the tie-class emission ranks by index in lane-major order on the blocked arrangement (or slot-major on a compacted array, which is index order there), and the paths that take a whole bin need no tie handling. Both requirements compile the same code and measured identical cycles in every cell pair of every batch. Sorted output is not inherent (post-sort of k). Signed zero: one tie class. NaN: unsupported by contract.

## 7. Open items

- Three correctness bugs were found and fixed during the session, each caught by the oracle battery, never by random input: lanes beyond 2^d buckets double counting in a 4-bit digit pass (an infinite loop), the items above the range dropped by the W3 compaction, and the degenerate bracket turning infinite items into NaN images (T60, found with the host model `model_device.py`, which reproduces the device arithmetic and should accompany any bracket code).
- Eight measurement batches were used: n6w1, w3a (its N6w control carried the T60 bug and it is kept as a batch without a card), n6w2, w3b, n1b, atom32b, atom32fb, atom32w2. Batches built and not measured: atom128b, w3d5b, w3nc, v3b, v3w2, atom32fw2, n6w1b.
- The GB200 only: every number is from one node and one day, a B200 confirmation of the kill cells (N 512 k 64 and N 2048 k 256) is a verifier's hour.
- The harness: nothing asked of the evaluation engineer. The adapters assume the harness separates calls by a __syncwarp (it does).
