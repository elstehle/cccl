### T?: a keys-only bitonic top-k that needs only the k-th largest value stops its final merge at stride k and takes the k-th value with one redux.min, saving log2(k) shuffle stages
applies to: every register-resident bitonic or merge network whose output is a threshold rather than a sorted list (N10, the threshold stage of W5 and N6 at warp scope, any keys-only W1 or N5 network followed by a rank pass), sub-warp and warp scope, 32-bit words (twiddled fp32, packed bf16, integer keys)
measured: N10 fp32 keys-only u32 network, eager tile order, same recursion and exchange, only the final merge of tile 0 and the threshold extraction differ. Complete merge plus one shuffle of position k - 1 (N10_u32) against the merge stopped after the stages with stride at least k plus redux.sync.min over positions [0, k) (N10_u32_trunc), B200 umbriel-b200-020 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness chain-slope latency, prefer_smaller_index, same lock section: N 32 k 4 570 against 504 cycles (2 stages saved), N 32 k 16 570 against 442 (4 stages), N 64 k 32 815 against 661 (5 stages), N 64 k 16 815 against 696, N 128 k 16 1238 against 1106, N 128 k 64 1110 against 933 (MaxK 64: six stages of which one in registers), N 256 k 16 2105 against 1966, N 256 k 64 1704 against 1566. About 30 to 35 cycles per skipped shuffle stage, the redux costs what the shuffle cost. Throughput 245 against 259 to 285 Gelem/s at N 32, 278 against 283 to 301 at N 64, within 1 to 4 percent above. evidence: cuobjdump -sass of the N 128 k 16 latency kernels: 142 SHFL against 150 (two network copies, four stages of stride 8, 4, 2, 1 removed per copy), 2 CREDUX.MIN, no SHFL.IDX for the threshold, no BSSY, 0 stack. approaches/N10/n10_detail.cuh (common_net::merge0 with MinStride, group_min).
how to use: After the last pairing of the eager loop (or the last level of the level loop) the surviving tile is a bitonic sequence of MaxK words. Run only the half-cleaner stages with stride MaxK/2 down to k: positions [0, k) then hold the k largest words (unsorted, bitonic) and their minimum is the k-th largest. Take it with one `__reduce_min_sync` over the full warp (identity 0xffffffff in the lanes outside [0, k) and, at sub-warp scope, in the lanes of the other logical warps, T37 and T40), in-lane `min` over the registers first when k spans several registers. The threshold is then warp uniform (a uniform register, T1) and needs no broadcast shuffle. Requires k a power of two (else finish the merge inside the top block of next_pow2(k) words), and gives up the sorted output, which a rank pass over the original items does not need.
caveats: Only the final merge can be truncated, every earlier tile sort and merge must complete for the pairing to be valid. Integer redux only on the sm_100 build, so float keys must be twiddled (which the min max network wants anyway) or the sm_100f redux.sync.min.f32 used (T2). One toolkit (13.4.92), one SM, eager and level loops of approaches/N10 only.
