### T?: recover the indices of a keys-only warp selection by ballot rank in index order, exact prefer_smaller_index for about 60 cycles at IPT 1 and 50 per further item per lane, one ballot per slot with the tie pass behind a uniform branch from IPT 4 on
applies to: every warp or sub-warp selection that ends with the k-th largest key known and the original keys still in registers (N10, the compare filter of W5, the bracket of N6 at warp scope, any keys-only W1 or N5 network, the per-warp finish of block methods), fp32 and other keys that have no room for an index next to them, both unsorted requirements
measured: N10 fp32 keys-only u32 network, B200 umbriel-b200-020 and -028 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness chain-slope latency, same lock section per comparison. Two-class pass (one "above t" and one "equal to t" ballot per slot, popc prefixes, one predicated store per item) after a complete merge against the identical u32 network with the index packed on bf16 words (W1x_vimnmx_eager, network only): 570 against 512 at N 32 (IPT 1), 815 against 695 at N 64, 1238 against 1017 at N 128 (IPT 4), 2105 against 1718 at N 256 (IPT 8), so 58, 120, 221, 387 cycles including the twiddle and the shuffle of position k - 1. One-ballot pass ("at least t" ballot per slot, prefix rank is the slot when the group holds exactly k words at least t, else a warp-uniform branch into the two-class pass with the "above" ballots added) against the two-class pass on the same truncated network: plus 65 at N 32 and plus 51 to 58 at N 64 (the branch and the popc sum cost more than the saved ballots), minus 53 to 81 at N 128 and minus 102 to 129 at N 256 (N10_ge1_trunc 1085, 1053, 1018, 1893, 1862, 1829 against N10_u32_trunc 1166, 1106, 1071, 2022, 1966, 1931 at N 128 and 256, k 4, 16, 32). Price of excess ties at rank k (patterns pivot_tie and all_equal against random, N10_auto): 0 at N 64 (two-class form), plus 100 at N 128 (1060 against 960), plus 264 at N 256 (1537 against 1273), the branch-free two-class control measured the same on every pattern (1104 to 1106, 1960 to 1966). evidence: cuobjdump -sass of the N 256 k 16 latency kernels. Two-class pass per slot: two ISETP, two VOTE.ANY, two LOP3, four POPC, four IADD, one ISETP, one IMAD, one predicated STS (about 19 instructions, 150 for IPT 8), ptxas interleaves the second ballot of a slot with the popc and add chains of the earlier slots, so the pass is issue bound at about 48 cycles per slot instead of the 6 cycles per further ballot of T17. One-ballot pass: eight VOTE.ANY back to back, POPC sum, ISETP against k, BSSY, predicated BRA, fast path with one POPC, LOP3, IADD, ISETP, IMAD, predicated STS per slot, the two-class block with eight more VOTE on the other side, BSYNC. Source approaches/N10/n10_detail.cuh (recover_two_class, recover_fast_path).
how to use: Keep the original keys (or their twiddled words) in registers across the network, get the k-th largest word t into a uniform register (redux or shuffle), then per slot i (slot-major, so slot i of lane l is index i x LW + l and index order is slot then lane): ballot the class predicate over the full warp, mask it to the logical warp's lanes, rank = popc(ballot & lanes_below_me) plus the running sum of popc(ballot) over the earlier slots, store the item's index at out[rank] under the class predicate. With one "at least t" ballot per slot the ranks are the slots whenever the ballots count exactly k (no excess ties at rank k, always true for random fp32), and a branch on the warp-uniform count (made uniform over the hardware warp with one any-vote at sub-warp scope) handles the tie case with IPT more "above t" ballots: above items first in index order, then the tied items in index order until k, which is prefer_smaller_index exactly and costs nothing extra under not_guaranteed. Use the two-class pass without the branch at IPT 1 and 2, the one-ballot pass from IPT 4 on. Expect 50 to 60 cycles per item per lane for the two-class pass and about half for the one-ballot pass at IPT 8, and check the SASS for the ballots being issued back to back.
caveats: Output in class and index order, not sorted by key. Requires all lanes of the hardware warp in the call (full-warp ballots and the uniform branch), which logical-warp primitives in a CUB header would have to state as a contract. The two-class pass does not reach the pipelined-ballot cost in the measured code, an asm barrier between the ballot block and the prefix block was not tried. One toolkit (13.4.92), one SM.
