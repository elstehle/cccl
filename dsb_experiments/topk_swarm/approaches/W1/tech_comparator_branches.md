### T?: a composite comparator written with || and && inside a predicated compare-exchange network becomes a branch region per exchange, written with | and & it stays predicated and runs 3.5x to 6x faster
applies to: every sort or selection network that takes a user comparator over a struct key (cub::detail::WarpBitonicTopK and WarpBitonicSort, WarpMergeSort, the W1, N2, W4 and B6 merge stages), key-then-index tie-break comparators above all, any key width
measured: W1_kvcmp against W1_baseline, the same keys-only cub::detail::WarpBitonicTopK<kv, MaxK, 32> instantiation on an (fp32 or bf16, int32) struct whose comparator is `a.k > b.k || (a.k == b.k && a.i < b.i)` in the control and `(a.k > b.k) | ((a.k == b.k) & (a.i < b.i))` in the variant, same lock section, prefer_smaller_index. B200 umbriel-b200-020 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness chain-slope latency: fp32 N 32 k 8 721 against 2541 cycles per call (3.5x), N 64 k 32 1081 against 5944 (5.5x), N 128 k 32 2120 against 12899 (6.1x), N 256 k 64 5902 against 33467 (5.7x); bf16 N 32 k 8 1072 against 1396 (1.30x), N 128 k 32 3400 against 5020 (1.48x), N 256 k 32 8338 against 13288 (1.59x). Throughput fp32 117 against 69 Gelem/s at N 32 and 98 against 52 at N 256 (1.7x to 1.9x), bf16 1.16x to 1.24x. Against the not_guaranteed pairs reference in the same session the branch-free struct form costs 1.03x to 1.24x latency for fp32 and 1.35x to 1.50x for bf16, the short-circuit form 4.4x to 6.4x and 1.8x to 2.4x. Cards W1_kvcmp_b200_20261025_1 and W1_baseline_b200_20261025_1, files state/results/opt-W1b/W1_kvcmp_vs_W1_baseline_focus_kv_nobranch_20261025_133840.json and W1_baseline_vs_W1_reference_focus_base_cub_psi_20261025_131351.json.
evidence: cuobjdump -sass of the fp32 N 128 k 32 latency kernels: the short-circuit form has 3448 instructions with 300 BSSY, 300 BSYNC and 305 BRA (a convergence region per comparator call, two calls per exchange of the 150-exchange network), 612 FSETP and 449 PLOP3; the bitwise form has 1840 instructions, no BSSY, BSYNC or BRA, 462 FSETP, 189 PLOP3 and 154 FSEL (the not_guaranteed reference with a single-key comparator: 1152). For bf16 neither form has branches (the __hgt and __heq intrinsics are branch-free), 2672 against 2280 instructions, and the 1.3x to 1.6x there is the predicate chain of the short circuit: PLOP3 455 against 189, P2R 58 against 23.
how to use: In a comparator that a network evaluates under predication, build the composite predicate from bools with | and & (or compute the two comparisons into bools first and combine), never with || and &&: cicc keeps the short circuit as control flow, ptxas wraps each in BSSY and BSYNC, and a fully unrolled network of N log^2 N exchanges serializes on the regions. The bitwise form evaluates the index compare unconditionally, one ISETP, which is far cheaper than the region. Check with `cuobjdump -sass <bin> | grep -c BSSY` on the kernel, a predicated network has none.
caveats: The branch-free struct comparator still evaluates two compares per exchange and shuffles two words, so it loses to the index-in-key packing wherever the index fits next to the key in one word (card W1_eager_same_b200_20261025_1: against the branch-free struct form the packed bf16 u32 word is 1.9x to 3.5x faster and the packed fp32 u64 word 1.2x to 1.3x, with 1.6x to 1.7x the throughput). One toolkit (13.4.92), one SM; older cicc versions may already hoist the short circuit, check the SASS.
