### T?: a packed bf16x2 (or u16x2) compare-exchange is two predicated HMNMX2 only when the two SIMD calls sit inside the ternary, the select of two computed results costs a third instruction and ptxas rebuilds the branch predicate per stage
applies to: N5 two-row bf16x2 networks, any bitonic or merge network on packed 16-bit keys (bf16x2 through `max.bf16x2` and `min.bf16x2`, u16x2 through `__vmaxu2` and `__vminu2`), every compare-exchange whose min and max come from inline PTX or from a header intrinsic that is inline PTX, sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92
measured: static SASS of a 32-stage shuffle exchange chain with a lane-dependent direction per stage (approaches/N5/micro/exchange_forms.cu, `nvcc -O3 -arch=sm_100 -cubin`, cuobjdump): u32 `keep ? max(k, o) : min(k, o)` (LLVM intrinsics) 128 instructions, 57 VIMNMX, 25 predicated, the predicated-overwrite pair of T42. bf16x2 with the two asm calls inside the ternary (`keep ? hmax2(k, o) : hmin2(k, o)`) 144 instructions, 57 HMNMX2, 50 predicated, 10 ISETP and 20 LOP3 against 1 and 10 for u32. bf16x2 with both results computed and then selected (`mx = hmax2(k, o); mn = hmin2(k, o); keep ? mx : mn`) 152 instructions, 57 HMNMX2 unpredicated, 27 IMAD.MOV (a predicated move per stage). The header intrinsics `__hmax2` and `__hmin2` on `__nv_bfloat162` give the same 152. u16x2 through `__vmaxu2` and `__vminu2`: 144 in the ternary form, 152 in the select form, the same two shapes. A LOP3 blend by a per-lane mask 152. `neg.bf16x2` or an xor of the sign bits on the partner operand is never folded into HMNMX2 (168 and 168 instructions, one LOP3 per negation). The predicate placed inside one asm block (`setp.ne.u32 p, %2, 0; @p max.bf16x2 ...; @!p min.bf16x2 ...`) is worst: 424 against 352 SASS instructions in the N 128 k 32 split kernel (the bool is materialized around every asm block with SEL, ISETP and IMAD.MOV). In the N5 adapters (B200 umbriel-b200-018, harness 0.5, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25): switching the two-row kernel from the select form to the ternary form took the N 128 k 32 throughput kernel from 576 to 528 SASS instructions (IMAD 128 to 51, ISETP 41 to 67) and the throughput from 445 to 458 Gelem per s (W1x_vimnmx_eager control 358 in both sections), with registers 32 to 48 and occupancy 8 to 5 blocks per SM. Batches state/results/opt-N5/N5_rows_vs_W1x_vimnmx_eager_focus_b1_20261025_153333.json and ..._b2_20261025_154745.json.
evidence: SASS of the micro, kernel `chain<f_bf16_asm>`: per stage `SHFL.BFLY`, `@P0 HMNMX2.BF16_V2 R6, R5, R4, !PT`, `@!P0 HMNMX2.BF16_V2 R6, R5, R4, PT`, then `LOP3.LUT R4, R0, R2, RZ, 0x3c` and `LOP3.LUT P0, R4, R4, 0x1, RZ, 0xc0` (two instructions to rebuild the next direction predicate from shifted lane copies, or `ISETP.NE.U32 P0, R9, RZ` on a materialized value). Kernel `chain<f_u32>`: `VIMNMX.U32 R7, PT, PT, R2, R3, !PT` then `@!P0 VIMNMX.U32 R7, PT, PT, R2, R3, PT` with the predicates `LOP3.LUT P3, RZ, R0, 0x1, R3, 0x48` computed once and kept in P registers. Kernel `chain<f_bf16_hdr>`: `HMNMX2 R19 = max`, `HMNMX2 R18 = min`, `@P0 IMAD.MOV.U32 R19, RZ, RZ, R18`. At sub-warp scope (LW 16, N 128, IPT 8) the same ternary source compiles to two predicated HMNMX2 into different registers plus two predicated IMAD.MOV (four instructions per stage word, kernel N5_rows_f_sub in the syn6 build), where the select form gives the deterministic three.
how to use: Write the packed exchange as `k = keep_max ? hmax2(k, other) : hmin2(k, other)` with the SIMD calls inside the ternary. The front end cannot speculate an inline asm result into a select, so it keeps a branch, and ptxas if-converts the two one-instruction arms into two predicated HMNMX2 that write the same register, the shape of the u32 route. Expect about half an extra instruction per stage for the predicate (ISETP or LOP3 to a predicate register per stage, since the if-converted predicate is not hoisted and reused like the select-derived one), so the packed stage is 2.5 to 2.65 instructions for two keys against 2.3 for one u32 key. Check the SASS for `IMAD.MOV` next to the HMNMX2 pairs: their presence means the select form (three per stage) or, at sub-warp instantiations, the four-instruction phi form, where the explicit select form is the better of the two. A per-lane direction mask or precomputed lane-bit parity words do not reduce the predicate cost (N5_rows_fd: identical counts). Nothing is gained by negating an operand: HMNMX2 has no free operand negation in this toolchain.
caveats: One toolkit (nvcc 13.4.92, x86 host) and one SM (sm_100). The if-conversion decision is ptxas's and depends on the surrounding code (the sub-warp case above), so re-read the SASS after every structural change (T28). Both pipes are half rate (T47), so the extra predicate instruction costs the same pipe time as a min or max when it is a LOP3, less when it is an ISETP.
