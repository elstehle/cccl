### T?: a lookahead refill test for a two-level per-lane list fires after one win unless the head holds four blocks of rounds, and the block control (one vote, two taken branches, runtime round indices) costs about 18 cycles per round at blocks of 2 on one warp
applies to: two-level per-lane lists in redux or atomic extraction rounds (N7, B2_twolevel at warp scope, any W2 variant with a refilled head), every round loop that checks a rare condition with a warp vote every few rounds, warp and sub-warp scope
measured: N7_twolevel (head of 8 sorted words per lane, unsorted remainder, W2 packed rounds, rolled loop over blocks of B rounds, one `__any_sync` per block at the top of the block deciding a refill after the block, the refill a write-back merge of every remainder block into the head), bf16 and fp32, B200 umbriel-b200-028 sm_100 nvcc 13.4.92 2026-10-25, harness latency chain slope against W2_packed_v3 in the same lock section. Blocks of 4 with the test "fewer than 8 valid head words" (card-less batch n7b): round 70, 106 and 190 cycles at IPT 16, 32 and 64 (slopes over k), bf16 N 1024 k 64 7182 against W2 7308. Blocks of 2 with the test "fewer than 4 valid" (card N7_twolevel_b200_20261025_1, batch n7c): round 45 to 58 (IPT 16), 47 to 71 (IPT 32), 44 to 74 (IPT 64), bf16 N 1024 k 64 4623 against 7308, k 128 8382 against 11872, N 2048 k 128 9451 against 21324, fp32 N 2048 k 32 2843 against 7353. W2's pruned-list round in the same kernels is 25 to 27 cycles (T19, T41).
evidence: the refill test at the top of block b must guarantee that no lane runs dry during block b + 1, so with B rounds per block it has to fire when a lane holds fewer than 2 B valid words at the end of block b - 1: with a head of 8 and B 4 that is any lane that has won once since its last refill, so the warp-uniform refill (sort and merge every remainder block, about 70 instructions per block of 8 words) ran in nearly every block and the per-round cost scaled with the tile (n7b SASS: 968 to 1000 instructions, no local memory, one copy of the refill). With B 2 the test needs five wins of one lane, the refill is rare, and the loop body per block of 2 rounds (SASS of the n7c throughput kernel at bf16 N 1024 k 32) is two CREDUX rounds (CREDUX, UR to R move, ISETP, 8 SEL, the emission ISETP, LOP3 and predicated STS with runtime indices), the two ISETP of the test, VOTE.ANY, a predicated BRA over the refill and the back-edge BRA: two taken branches per block, and the round measures 45 to 74 cycles against 27.
how to use: Size a refilled head as at least four times the block of rounds between votes (head 8 with blocks of 2, head 16 with blocks of 4 at the price of the deeper shift, T19) or the refill is not rare. Budget the block control at about 35 cycles per block on one warp (the vote issue, two taken branches, the loop counter and the runtime emission indices), so a two-level list with blocks of 2 costs about 45 cycles per round and only pays against a plain sorted list deeper than 16 (T19: 37 at 16, 89 at 32, 147 at 64). Prefer a design that avoids per-block control altogether (a prefilter that bounds the list depth, W5) where the list depth is the problem.
caveats: One toolkit, one SM, one warp per scheduler (in the throughput regime the branches and the vote overlap with other warps and N7's throughput is 1.26x to 2.64x over W2 at k 32 to 128). The 18 cycles per round were not separated into the taken-branch and the vote shares, a layout with the common path falling through (refill out of line) and two blocks per loop iteration was not measured.
