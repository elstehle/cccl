### T?: in k extraction rounds over a per-lane sorted list the compiler drops the shifts of positions that cannot reach the head, so the list depth is min(k, items per lane) and the round stays at the IPT 8 cost at any N
applies to: W2, B2 and B5 extraction rounds (redux or atomic), any k-round loop that pops the head of a register-resident sorted list with predicated shifts, all key widths
measured: W2 packed rounds inside the full primitive (prologue, k rounds, emission, harness chain), B200 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, umbriel-b200-068 and umbriel-b200-091, state/results/opt-W2/W2_packed_vs_W1_reference_focus_slope_20261025_125428.json and ..._grid_a_20261025_133656.json, card W2_packed_b200_20261025_1.json. Slope over k at N 256 (IPT 8): 29.6 cycles per round from k 8 to 16, 24.9 from k 16 to 32. At N 1024 (IPT 32) with k 8 the list is 8 deep and the whole primitive costs 1061 cycles (round about 27), where T19 measured 89 per round for a 32-deep list shifted in full. At N 1024 k 16 (list 16) 1607, k 32 (list 32) 2674, k 64 (list 32, every round shifts the whole list) 6969, which is the 89-cycle round of T19 plus the k 64 merge.
evidence: SASS SEL counts of the throughput kernel: 28 SEL for 8 rounds on a list of 8 at N 256 k 8 (7 + 6 + ... + 0, position i is shifted only in rounds 0 to L - 2 - i after unrolling), 120 for 16 rounds on a list of 16, against 8 x 7 = 56 and 16 x 15 = 240 for unpruned shifts. The rounds must be a fully unrolled loop with a compile-time k (the adapter rolls the loop by 4 above k 64 and the pruning is lost there). Files harness/adapters/W2_impl.cuh (`select_top`, `extract_rounds`), approaches/W2/sass_funcs.py.
how to use: Size the per-lane list as L = next power of two of min(k, items per lane), select the L largest of the lane's items in the prologue (a sort of L followed by (IPT / L - 1) sorted top-L merges, 390 VIMNMX for IPT 32 at L 16) and run the k rounds fully unrolled. The round then costs 25 to 30 cycles for any N as long as k is at most 32, and the N dependence moves entirely into the per-lane prologue (about 500 cycles at IPT 32, 1200 at IPT 64 for bf16). For k above the list depth every round shifts the whole list and the T19 numbers apply (50 cycles at L 16, 89 at L 32, 147 at L 64).
caveats: Unrolled rounds only, k a compile-time constant. The prologue network grows with IPT x log2 L, so above IPT 32 a two-level list (sorted top L plus an unsorted remainder refilled by a lane max) or a prefilter should replace the full sort-and-merge prologue, not measured. The pruning was observed with nvcc 13.4.92 at -O3, a change of the shift form (branch instead of select) loses it (T19).
