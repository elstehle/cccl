### T?: redux.sync on a sub-warp member mask is lowered to a WARPSYNC loop over the distinct masks, one full-warp redux per logical warp with the identity in the other lanes is 4 to 7 times faster
applies to: every sub-warp (logical warp 4, 8, 16) collective on the critical path, W2 and B5 sub-warp phases, W1 and N2 sub-warp variants, anything that reaches `__reduce_*_sync` with a per-group mask (cub::WarpReduce at a logical width below 32 included)
measured: W2 packed extraction rounds, bf16 N 32 k 4 to 16, cycles per round from the slope over k. Masked `redux.sync` on the member mask: T 16 (2 logical warps per hardware warp) 174, T 8 (4 per warp) 327, T 4 (8 per warp) 649, against 25 at T 32. One full-warp `__reduce_max_sync(0xffffffff, group == g ? v : 0)` per logical warp plus a select of the own group's result: T 16 35, T 8 34.5, T 4 88. Whole primitive, bf16: T 8 N 32 k 4 1433 against 295 cycles (4.9x), T 4 N 32 k 16 10619 against 1571 (6.8x), T 16 N 128 k 16 3162 against 782 (4.0x), T 8 N 128 k 8 (gpt-oss and Qwen3 router shape) 3152 against 802 (3.9x), fp32 within 10 percent of the same ratios. Throughput regime 1.1x to 1.7x (the serialized passes of the masked form overlap across the 8 warps of a block). B200 = NVIDIA B200 sm_100, umbriel-b200-047 (A/B of the two forms in one lock section), nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, state/results/opt-W2/W2_packed_sw_v2_vs_W2_packed_sw_focus_sw_v2_20261025_140113.json, cards W2_packed_sw_b200_20261025_1.json (baseline, umbriel-b200-091) and W2_packed_sw_v2_b200_20261025_1.json.
evidence: SASS of the masked form (adapter W2_packed_sw, T 8 N 32 k 4, throughput kernel): 8 `CREDUX.MAX` inside 5 `BSSY`, `WARPSYNC`, `BRA`, `BSYNC` regions, one region per round, the loop iterates over the distinct mask values present in the warp because the member mask differs per logical warp and the compiler emits the generic divergent-mask lowering of the collective. Full-warp form (W2_packed_sw_v2): 32 `CREDUX.MAX` (4 per round at 4 groups) with 17 plain `WARPSYNC` and 2 `BSSY`, no loop. Registers 26 against 28 for that kernel, 40 against 32 at T 8 N 128 k 64. Files harness/adapters/W2_impl.cuh (`masked_reducer`, `fullwarp_group_reducer`), approaches/W2/sass_funcs.py for the counts.
how to use: In a logical-warp primitive never hand a per-group mask to `__reduce_*_sync`. Issue G = 32 / T full-warp redux per collective, feeding the identity (0 for max, all ones for min, 0 for add) from the lanes of the other groups, and pick the own group's result with a select. The G redux pipeline at about 6 cycles each (T17), so a round costs about 22 + 6 (G - 1) plus 2 G selects: 35 measured at G 2 and G 4, 88 at G 8. At G 8 a two-step shuffle tree (about 60 cycles, T1) may be cheaper, not measured. The same check (BSSY and WARPSYNC around a collective in the SASS) applies to ballot and shuffle with per-group masks, which were not measured.
caveats: nvcc 13.4.92, sm_100 build. The lowering may differ when only one logical warp is active per hardware warp or on other toolkits. Latency regime at one warp per scheduler, the throughput gain is smaller because the masked form's passes overlap across warps. The identity selects cost about 2 registers per group.
