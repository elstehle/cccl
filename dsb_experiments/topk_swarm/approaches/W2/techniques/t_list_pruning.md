### T?: with fully unrolled rounds the compiler drops the shifts of list positions that cannot reach the head, so a per-lane sorted list costs the IPT 8 round at any list depth up to k and the T19 cliff above IPT 8 moves to k above the list depth
applies to: W2 and B5 extraction rounds and every per-lane sorted candidate list consumed head first for a compile-time number of rounds (B2 per-thread lists, N1 and W3 slot emission), all key widths
measured: W2_packed warp primitive, harness latency chain slope, B200 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, umbriel-b200-068 and 091, batches state/results/opt-W2/W2_packed_vs_W1_reference_focus_slope_20261025_125428.json and W2_packed_vs_W1_reference_focus_grid_a_20261025_133656.json (card W2_packed_b200_20261025_1.json): slope over k at N 256 (IPT 8, list depth 8) 24.9 cycles per round from k 16 to 32, N 1024 k 8 with a list of 8 items selected from 32 per lane 1061 cycles at 41 registers (the rounds at about 27 each, the rest is the selection network), N 2048 k 4 (IPT 64, list 4) 1157 cycles at 39 registers. T19 measured 89 cycles per round at IPT 32 with every list entry kept live. With k above the list depth every round shifts the whole list: N 512 k 256 (list 16) 50 cycles per round, N 2048 k 1024 (list 64) 147 per round.
evidence: SASS of the throughput kernels (approaches/W2/sass_funcs.py over the batch binaries): 28 SEL for 8 rounds on a list of 8 (7 + 6 + ... + 0 shifts), 120 SEL for 16 rounds on a list of 16, one CREDUX per round, no VOTE, no local memory. T19's micro kept the whole list live with a final fold, which is the k at or above list depth case.
how to use: Give each lane a sorted list of depth L = min(k, items per lane) built by a selection network (sorted blocks of L and pairwise top-L merges), unroll the k rounds fully (k up to 64 compiles in seconds), write the shift as selects, and let the compiler prune. Round r then shifts min(L - 1, k - 1 - r) positions and the round stays at 25 to 27 cycles for any items per lane as long as k is at most 16. The cost moves to the selection network, which is issue bound at about one instruction per cycle on one warp (bitonic blocks: 48 instructions for 8 items, 160 for 16, odd-even merge sort blocks 20 percent fewer, see the W2 v3 card). Keep the list live after the rounds only when something reads it (the fp32 next-word check reads the head), that costs one more live position per round.
caveats: Holds for a compile-time k with the round loop unrolled. A rolled round loop (W2 uses one above k 64) keeps every position live and pays the full-list shift. The selection network for L above 16 and items per lane above 32 is the dominant cost and the T34 unrolling limit applies to it.
