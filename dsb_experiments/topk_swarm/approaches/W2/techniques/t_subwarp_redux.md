### T?: redux.sync on a sub-warp member mask is lowered to a WARPSYNC loop over the distinct masks, about 85 cycles per logical warp per call, use one full-warp redux per group with the identity in the other lanes instead
applies to: every sub-warp (logical warp 4, 8, 16) collective written with `__reduce_*_sync(member_mask, v)` or `redux.sync` with a per-group mask on sm_100, W2 and B5 sub-warp phases, N2 and W1 sub-warp variants that reduce, any grouped MoE routing primitive
measured: W2 packed extraction, one hardware warp holding 32 / T logical warps of T lanes, harness latency chain slope, bf16 N 32: slope over k with the masked redux is 174 cycles per round at T 16 (2 groups), 327 at T 8 (4 groups), 649 at T 4 (8 groups), against 25 per round for the same code at T 32. With one full-warp `__reduce_max_sync(0xffffffff, group == g ? v : 0)` per group g and a select of the own group's result the round costs 34 at T 16, 46 at T 8 and 88 at T 4 (k 8 to 16 slopes), whole-primitive cells T 8 N 32 k 4 295 against 1433 cycles, T 4 N 8 k 4 369 against 2636, T 16 N 128 k 16 782 against 3162, fp32 T 8 N 128 k 8 1184 against 3883 (2.6x to 7.1x over 46 cells, both variants in one lock section). B200 sm_100, umbriel-b200-047 and umbriel-b200-091, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, files state/results/opt-W2/W2_packed_sw_v2_vs_W2_packed_sw_focus_sw_v2_20261025_140035.json and W2_packed_sw_vs_W2_packed_sw_focus_subwarp_20261025_133928.json, cards W2_packed_sw_b200_20261025_1.json and W2_packed_sw_v2_b200_20261025_1.json.
evidence: cuobjdump of the T 8 N 32 k 4 throughput kernel of W2_packed_sw: the CREDUX sits inside a `BSSY B0`, `WARPSYNC R9`, `BRA` back-edge, `BSYNC B0` region (the compiler's lowering of a collective whose mask is not provably warp uniform: one pass per distinct mask value, the lanes of the other groups wait). CREDUX writes a uniform register (T1), so a per-group result cannot come out of one instruction. The sw_v2 kernel has 32 CREDUX for 4 rounds at 4 groups, no WARPSYNC loop, and the independent CREDUX pipeline at about 6 cycles each (T17), so the round is 22 + 6 (G - 1) plus 2 G selects.
how to use: In lockstep logical warps never pass a per-group mask to redux.sync. Issue the collective for every group over the full warp with the identity element (0 for max and add, all ones for min) in the lanes of the other groups, and pick the own group's result with a select chain. The G reductions are independent and pipeline, so the cost is one redux latency plus about 6 cycles per further group. The same holds for `__reduce_add_sync` and `__reduce_min_sync` in the tie pass. Shuffle trees (log2 T steps of 30 cycles) cost about the same as the pipelined redux only at T 4 and more at T 8 and 16.
caveats: One toolkit (nvcc 13.4.92). At T 4 the eight full-warp reductions cost 88 cycles per round, so a shuffle-xor tree of two steps (about 72) may still be preferable there, not measured. The trick assumes all groups of the hardware warp run the same round count in lockstep (true for a fixed k). Block-wide the per-scheduler CREDUX budget of T33 applies (G redux per round per warp).
