### T?: the same large straight-line kernel at one warp per scheduler costs 25 to 33 percent more cycles on one B200 node than on two others, while every kernel under about 50 KB and every two-warp-per-scheduler kernel costs the same on all three
applies to: fully unrolled block kernels of more than about 64 KB of SASS launched with 128 threads (one warp per scheduler), B2 at 128 threads with k 64, any large unrolled network or round loop measured in the latency regime on more than one node; the verifier's node choice for such cards
measured: B2_packed at 128 threads, N 4096, k 64 (same binary, B200 sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, harness latency chain slope, B1_reference at 128 threads as the same-section control): bf16 6254 cycles on umbriel-b200-047 (card B2_packed_b200_20261025_3, two repeats 6254 and 6254), 6056 on umbriel-b200-068 (batch state/results/opt-N8/N8_b2packed_vs_B1_reference_n8_ns068_20261025_152940.json), 7831 on umbriel-b200-020 (verify-B2 batch B2_packed_vs_B1_reference_focus_vt128_20261025_142108, and 7831 again in opt-N8 batch N8_b2packed_vs_B1_reference_n8_ns020_20261025_154336); fp32 6219, 6205, 8267 and 8264 (the two -020 batches). The controls on the three nodes: 6074, 6062, 6075 (bf16) and 7055, 7039, 7086 (fp32). The 256-thread siblings of the same shape (7290 bf16, 7686 fp32, 3064 and 3728 instructions, two warps per scheduler) agree on all three nodes to 0.3 percent, as do the k 16 cells at 128 threads (1907 bf16, 1536 instructions) and the k 32 cells (3205 bf16 at 2576 instructions, 3394 to 3439 fp32 at 4120 instructions, 40 and 64 KB, identical on -068 and -020 to 1.3 percent). Throughput of the same kernels agrees to 0.5 percent (259 and 208 Gelem per s at k 64 on every node). SM clock during the batches 1965 MHz on every busy sample on both -068 and -020 (nvidia-smi 100 ms trace, state/results/opt-N8/smi_ns068_umbriel-b200-068.csv and smi_ns020_umbriel-b200-020.csv, memory 3996 MHz, no event reason beyond idle), same SKU, VBIOS, ECC state and driver on all nodes (hw-researcher inventory).
evidence: the affected kernels are 5008 (bf16) and 6552 (fp32) SASS instructions, 78 and 102 KB of straight-line code per launch at 48 and 128 registers with 0 spill, one block of 4 warps on one SM; the unaffected kernels of the same batch are at most 4120 instructions (64 KB) at one warp per scheduler or 3728 instructions at two warps per scheduler. The chain minima on -020 are a uniform 24 to 33 percent higher at every chain length (a steady per-call cost, not a cold-cache one-off, verify-B2's and this card's raw tables). The hardware researcher's straight-line code-size sweep (4 to 128 KB, 1 to 16 warps) found no fetch cost on -047 and -091 and the -020 run of that probe is the open item that names the mechanism. Card N8_b2packed_b200_20261025_3.json holds both nodes' cells (94 of 96 identical to 0.7 percent, the two k 64 kernels at 1.29x and 1.33x).
how to use: Treat latency numbers of a fully unrolled kernel above about 64 KB at 128 threads as node dependent until measured on two nodes, and say which node in the card. For the final evaluation measure such cells on a node that agrees with the majority (-047 and -068 here) or roll the round loop (B2 above k 64 already uses `#pragma unroll 1`, B2b report 3.5), which also removes the instruction-cache pressure. Do not derive a crossover from one such cell on one node: card 3's "k 64 ties B1 at N 4096" holds on two nodes and fails on the third, and against B1 at 256 threads the cell loses on every node, so the block size does not move B2's crossover at k 64.
caveats: Three nodes, one toolchain, one kernel family (B2's unrolled rounds); the threshold between 64 KB (unaffected) and 78 KB (affected) at one warp per scheduler is bracketed, not bisected; whether the effect is instruction fetch from L2, an SM-local cache configuration or a per-GPU firmware setting is unknown (hw-researcher). GB200 not measured.
