### T?: correction to T43, the per-warp term of the block round is the barrier alone (2 cycles per warp) and the aggregated post is flat, the rest of the round growth is the list shift
applies to: the same rounds as T43 (B2 and every post, barrier, read back, shift round at block scope), block sizes 64 to 512
measured: hw-researcher on umbriel-b200-091 (B200, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, chain slope at one block, rows under state/results/hw-researcher/umbriel-b200-091-blocksize/): __syncthreads 15.9, 20.0, 28.0, 44.5 cycles at 2, 4, 8, 16 warps (about 12 plus 2 per warp); the aggregated atomic round without a list (atomicMax into slot[n] from every thread, barrier, LDS) 61.7, 65.7, 74.5, 91.5, which is the barrier plus a flat 46; the classic redux round 81.1, 84.9, 93.0, 109.2, barrier plus a flat 65. Against B2_packed's measured rounds (T43, umbriel-b200-020): 83.5 at 8 warps and IPT 4 is 9 cycles above the list-free round, 69.5 at 4 warps and IPT 8 is 4 above, 77 at 2 warps and IPT 16 is 15 above, 91 at 8 warps and IPT 8 is 17 above, 78 at 4 warps and IPT 16 is 12 above, 80 at 2 warps and IPT 32 is 18 above, 175 at 16 warps and IPT 16 is 83 above.
evidence: the list-free round drops 4 cycles from 4 to 2 warps and B2's round rises 8, so the 2-warp loss is the shift of a 16-word list on the critical path (T19 measured the packed list round at 26 cycles up to IPT 8 and 37 at IPT 16, the same 11 cycles), not the barrier and not the post count. The 16-warp B2 round at IPT 16 (175) exceeds the list-free 16-warp round (91.5) by 83 cycles, more than the shift explains, which stays open (the 16 aggregated posts of a 512-thread block land on one slot address, see T10 for the serialized form).
how to use: Read T43's fit 48 + 4 W + IPT as barrier (12 + 2 W) plus a flat 46 for the aggregated post and read back plus a shift term that is about IPT at 2 to 8 warps for a fully unrolled list. The block size lever for B2 is therefore the barrier only (4 cycles per halving) and the list depth per thread costs it back one for one; the levers that remain are a per-thread list of at most 8 (T38 pruning) with more warps, or a two-level list. T11's measured line carries the new rows.
caveats: Two nodes (the list-free rounds on umbriel-b200-091, the B2 rounds on umbriel-b200-020), the same toolchain and arch; T16 found per-SM cycle costs identical across B200 nodes for every micro measured, and verify-B2 found node dependence only for 80 to 105 KB unrolled kernels at one warp per scheduler, which the IPT 4 to 16 rounds here are not.
