### T?: the aggregated block round costs about 48 + 4 W + IPT cycles at W warps per block, so 2-warp blocks gain nothing over 4-warp blocks for a round that shifts a per-thread list
applies to: B2 and every block-scope round of the form post (fire-and-forget atomic, aggregated to one CREDUX plus one atomic per warp, T26), barrier, read back, predicated shift of an IPT-word register list; block sizes 64 to 256, both key types
measured: B2_packed round slope from consecutive k in the latency regime, bf16 N 1024 (N = 32 W IPT): 83.5 cycles at 8 warps and IPT 4 (k 4 to 64: 84.1, 83.0, 85.0, 83.0), 69.5 at 4 warps and IPT 8 (68.5, 70.0, 68.6, 69.5), 77 at 2 warps and IPT 16 (88.0, 77.0, 72.9, 66.6); N 2048: 91 at 8 warps and IPT 8, 78 at 4 warps and IPT 16, 80 at 2 warps and IPT 32; fp32 within 5 cycles. Whole call bf16 N 1024 k 16: 1380, 1182, 1422 cycles at 256, 128, 64 threads (fp32 1680, 1494, 1724), the 64-thread form predicted at 1060 by a 58-cycle round model (ideas/queue/N8_block_size_for_round_based_primitives.md) is 0.83x of the 128-thread form. B200 sm_100, umbriel-b200-020, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25, one lock section, card N8_b2packed_b200_20261025_1.json, batch state/results/opt-N8/N8_b2packed_vs_N8_b1idx_n8_b2t_20261025_145019.json.
evidence: the same source (harness/adapters/B2_packed.cuh, inherited by N8_b2packed.cuh) at three block sizes, SASS 224, 312, 504 instructions for the k 4 latency kernel with 15 BAR.SYNC in each, 0 STL and LDL, registers 21, 29, 32; the round is LDS, ISETP, SEL x (IPT - 1), CREDUX.MAX, IMAD.U32, predicated ATOMS.MAX, BAR.SYNC and the shift selects are issued in program order before the next post. A fit of the six rounds above: 48 + 4 W + IPT, residual at most 8 cycles.
how to use: Model the block round as a per-warp term (barrier plus one aggregated post, about 4 cycles per warp) plus a per-list-word term (one cycle per predicated select, issued in order on a warp that holds one of the chain's dependents) plus a constant (CREDUX, atomic, LDS, compare). For a fixed N the sum 4 W + N / (32 W) is smallest at W near sqrt(N / 128), 2 to 4 warps at N 1024, 4 at N 2048, 4 to 8 at N 4096, and the prologue sort of IPT words (63 pairs at 16, 191 at 32, 543 at 64) decides the ties toward the larger block. Do not go below 4 warps for a round that shifts a list; a two-level list (head of 4) removes the IPT term but B2_twolevel's refill branch cost more than the shift it saved at IPT 16 (B2b report 3.3).
caveats: One node, latency regime, bf16 and fp32 packed words, k at most 64, IPT at most 32 for the fit (at IPT 64 the round is 179 cycles at 2 warps because the list no longer fits the pruning of T38 for k 64). The barrier cost at 2 warps is not in the catalogue (T11 has 128, 256 and 512 threads), so the 4 cycles per warp lump the barrier and the post. The throughput regime inverts the verdict (see the entry on block size and one-wave throughput).
