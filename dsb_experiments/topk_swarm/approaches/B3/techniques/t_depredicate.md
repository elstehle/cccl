### T?: ptxas wraps guarded shared-memory atomics in BSSY, BRA, BSYNC regions once a tile loop is long, also when they are written as predicated red.shared in inline PTX, guarded stores stay predicated
applies to: B1, B3 and every radix pass or histogram over a register tile with a per-item filter, block and warp scope
measured: static SASS of the harness latency kernels at IPT 64 (fp32 N 16384 k 64, 256 threads, sm_100, nvcc 13.4.92, 2026-10-25): B1_reference 516 BSSY, 527 BRA, 640 ATOMS of which 0 predicated, 324 STS. B3_padded 764 BSSY, 608 ATOMS 0 predicated, 880 STS of which 876 predicated. B3_straight, the same with `setp` plus `@p red.shared.add.u32` in one asm statement for every histogram increment and asm `@p st.shared` for every store: 482 BSSY, 608 ATOMS still 0 predicated (SASS per item: ISETP, BSSY, @P0 BRA, SHF, LOP3, ATOMS.POPC.INC.32, NOP, BSYNC), 876 predicated STS, registers 185 against 223, latency 1.00x to 1.10x on the compaction cells and 0.88x to 0.98x on bf16 fallback cells (registers 64 to 80 at N 8192, 96 spill bytes at N 16384), throughput 0.77x to 1.1x. B3_lean, plain C++ with one store per if and select increments: 494 BSSY like its control, 0.92x to 1.06x, registers 128 to 215 at bf16 N 16384 (per-item 64-bit destination pointers kept live). Files state/results/opt-B3/B3_straight_vs_B3_padded_b3_str3_20261025_132825.json and B3_lean_vs_B3_digit9_b3_lean5_20261025_133648.json, node umbriel-b200-047 (B200).
evidence: cuobjdump -sass of the cells above, counts by approaches/B3 sass statistics (BSSY, `@P ATOMS`, `@P STS` per kernel). At IPT 4 the July AIR report saw predicated fire-and-forget ATOMS, at IPT 64 the same source gets a reconvergence region per item in both primitives.
how to use: Do not expect a per-item `if (match) atomicAdd` to predicate on long unrolled tiles, and do not reach for inline PTX to force it, ptxas undoes it. Each region costs about four instructions per item per pass (BSSY, BRA, NOP, BSYNC) on top of the address computation it sinks inside, 256 instructions per thread per pass at IPT 64. Guarded stores do predicate, so keep stores as plain `if (c) *p = v`. For the atomics the remaining options are an unconditional atomic into a per-thread dummy word (256 private words issue at about 9 cycles per warp instruction, T10) or fewer guarded items, which is what compaction achieves. Avoid per-item generic pointers in such loops, they double the live registers.
caveats: One toolchain (nvcc 13.4.92) and one SM (sm_100). The threshold where ptxas switches from predication to regions was not located, it lies between IPT 4 and IPT 32. The dummy-word form is proposed, not measured.
