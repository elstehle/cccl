# N7: Two-level per-lane list for the packed W2 rounds at warp scope (k above the pruned list depth)
status: queued (wave 2, same optimizer as W5, they compete for the same cells)
scope: warp, subwarp
origin: new, from the wave 1 W2 cards (T38: the round stays at 25 to 30 cycles while the list depth min(k, IPT) is at most 8, 50 at depth 16, 89 at 32, 147 at 64) and opt-B2b's B2_twolevel card at block scope (sorted head of H plus an unsorted remainder with refill, fixed cost down by 230 to 1030 cycles, throughput up 9 to 58 percent, latency round up 27 to 80 cycles from a divergent refill branch per round).
hypothesis: W2's round is 26 cycles only while the compiler can prune the per-lane shifts, which needs k at most the pruned depth 8. Above that every round shifts the whole list: W2_packed_v3 measures N 1024 k 64 6628 cycles (about 90 per round) and N 2048 k 64 9237 (about 140 per round), N 2048 k 256 40417. Keep a sorted head of H 8 words per lane and the remainder unsorted. The round pops the head (26 cycles, 8 shifts pruned as today). A lane's head drains only after H wins, and the expected wins per lane are k over 32 (2 at k 64), so a refill is rare on random input. Check the heads once every H rounds with one full-warp ballot (27 cycles per H rounds, about 3 per round) and refill under a warp-uniform branch (a per-lane selection of the next H largest from the remainder, about IPT times H compares, rare). The round then costs about 30 cycles for any k, and the prologue is a top-H selection per lane (the B2_twolevel method: sorted groups of 4 merged in a tree keeping the H largest, 408 VIMNMX at IPT 32 against 644 for the full sort) instead of a full sort plus top-L merges.
cells it should win: warp N 512 to 2048 with k 64 to 128, bf16 and fp32 (fp32 through N4's packed word), where W2 today pays the deep list and W5 pays a top-2 bound. k 256 at N 1024 to 2048 against N6 warp is the boundary.

what it must beat (W2_packed_v3 and v1, B200, 2026-10-25, cycles bf16 then fp32): N 512 k 64 2704 and 4599, k 256 12362 and 12614. N 1024 k 32 2481 and 3059, k 64 6628 and 5917. N 2048 k 32 3970 and 6449, k 64 9237 and 8097, k 256 40417 and 42931. W5 predicted 2740 at N 512 k 64, 2900 at N 1024 k 64, 3220 at N 2048 k 64 (and it cannot bound k 128 cheaply). N6 warp predicted 2750 at N 1024 k 128 and 4700 at N 2048 k 256. To matter N7 has to be 2x under W2 at N 1024 k 64 (at most 3300) and within 10 percent of W5 there.

cost model (measured constants: round 26 at depth at most 8 (T38, W2 cards), ballot 27 (T17), B2_twolevel prologue savings and refill costs at block scope, VIMNMX 6 cycles T7):
- Prologue: top-H selection per lane, about 5 IPT over 4 plus the merge tree, about 300 cycles at IPT 32 and 500 at IPT 64 (B2_twolevel measured 400 against 968 fixed cost at IPT 32 and 234 against 1264 at IPT 64, at block scope with two warps per scheduler).
- Rounds: k times (26 + 27 over H + 2 for the head select of the two-level structure), about 30 per round at H 8.
- Refills: expected number per call about k over (32 H) per lane summed, rare on random input, each about IPT times H compares (about 300 at IPT 32). On sorted blocked input one lane wins every round and refills every H rounds, k over H refills, which is about the cost of today's deep-list W2, so the worst case is not worse than the incumbent.
- Totals (bf16, fp32 plus about 100 for N4's tie check): N 512 k 64 about 150 + 200 + 1920 = 2270 (W2 2704, W5 2740). N 1024 k 64 about 150 + 300 + 1920 = 2370 (W2 6628, W5 2900). N 2048 k 64 about 150 + 500 + 1920 = 2570 (W2 9237, W5 3220). N 1024 k 128 about 4300 (W2 above 10k, W3 4360 bf16 and 8700 fp32, N6 warp 2750). N 2048 k 256 about 8300 (W2 40417, N6 warp 4700).
- Throughput: B2_twolevel gained 9 to 58 percent at block scope from the shorter prologue and the pruned shifts, the same is expected here, W2 v3 is at 204 to 257 Gelem per s at k 64.

focus cells: 1. warp N 1024 k 64 bf16 (the kill cell), 2. warp N 2048 k 64 fp32, 3. warp N 512 k 64 bf16, 4. warp N 1024 k 128 bf16 (against N6 warp), 5. sub-warp LW 8 N 128 k 64 bf16 with the full-warp redux of T37 (W2 sw_v2 5261, N1 predicted 3000).
boundary cells: warp N 1024 k 32 (W2 2481 with the pruned list already, expect a tie), warp N 2048 k 256 (N6 warp expected to win 1.7x).

explore first: (1) H 8 head with the refill check every H rounds by one ballot and a warp-uniform branch, at N 1024 k 64 bf16 against W2_packed_v3 and W5 in one session. (2) H 4 against 8 against 16 (the pruned depth is 8 at nvcc 13.4, T38, confirm it holds for the head). (3) the prologue: B2_twolevel's group sort and merge tree against a direct top-H selection network. (4) the refill on sorted blocked input (worst case) and bf16 tie floods. (5) the unroll policy of the rounds above k 64 (W2's rolled loop above k 64 loses the pruning).

determinism and ordering: inherent (packed word), sorted output, prefer_smaller_index free as in W2.

main risk: at block scope the refill branch cost 27 to 80 cycles per round in the latency regime because ptxas put a BSSY and BSYNC region and an election into every round. At warp scope the check every H rounds with a warp-uniform branch is designed to avoid that, but T28 says the schedule of a long unrolled single block is fragile and the first build may not show the 30-cycle round.

cheapest kill experiment: explore item 1. If N7 is not at least 2x under W2_packed_v3 at N 1024 k 64 bf16 (at most 3300 cycles) with the refill never taken on random input, the structure is not paying and W5 owns the band.
