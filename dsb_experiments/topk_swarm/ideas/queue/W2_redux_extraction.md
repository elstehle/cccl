# W2: Redux based iterative extraction (warp and sub-warp)
status: queued (wave 1)
scope: warp, subwarp
origin: `reference/BLOCK_TOPK_RESULTS.md` section 1 (warp round 82 cycles, value-only keys with ballot and elect) and `hier_extract`, #9747 strategy 2
hypothesis: k rounds of `redux.sync` max. For bf16 pack key and inverted index into the 32-bit word so every word is unique and every round is an exact arg-max: the lane holding the maximum recognizes it by comparing its own head with the redux result, so there is no ballot, no ffs and no elect in the round. The round is the redux latency plus one compare and one select, measured at 26 cycles up to 8 items per lane (`TECHNIQUES.md` T19), against 74 for a block atomic round. Cost is linear in k and almost flat in N up to the point where the per-lane list shift exhausts issue bandwidth, so W2 owns small and medium k at any N a warp holds, which is the MoE router shape of 2026 (N 256 to 896 experts, k 6 to 22, see `ideas/WORKLOADS.md`).
cells it should win: warp and sub-warp, N 32 to 1024, k 4 to 32, bf16 first, fp32 with N4, prefer_smaller_index free in packed form. Sub-warp with 4 or 8 rows per hardware warp for the small routers (N 8 to 64).

cost model (measured 2026-10-25 by the hardware researcher on B200 and GB200, identical, nvcc 13.4.92, `TECHNIQUES.md` T2, T4, T17, T19: redux.sync u32 22.1 dependent, dependent ballot 26.7, __ffs 40, __popc 23, and the full packed list round itself in T19. July placeholders only where marked):
- Packed round, measured with a per-lane sorted list and predicated shifts (T19): 25.6 at IPT 1, 25.8 at IPT 4 and 8, 37.2 at IPT 16, 89.4 at IPT 32. ptxas issues the head select and the next CREDUX immediately and hides up to about 8 shifts behind the 22-cycle redux, above that the round is issue bound at about 2.8 cycles per item. Model: round(IPT) 26 for IPT at most 8, about 26 + 0.7 (IPT minus 8) to IPT 16, about 2.8 IPT at IPT 32 and above (180 at IPT 64, 360 at IPT 128, extrapolated). Shift as a branch on the winner is 34.5 at IPT 8 and 172 at IPT 32, never do that. Registers 25 at IPT 1, 45 at IPT 8, 64 at IPT 32, no spills.
- Prologue: twiddle and pack 2 instructions per item, per-lane sorting network of IPT items (about 40 cycles at IPT 4, 60 at IPT 8, July placeholder), about 100 in total.
- Packed bf16 cost about 100 + k round(IPT). N 32 k 8: 310. N 256 k 8: 310. N 256 k 16: 520. N 256 k 32: 930. N 256 k 64: 1760. N 512 k 16: 690. N 512 k 32: 1280. N 1024 k 16: 1520. N 1024 k 32: 2950. N 4096 k 16: 5860.
- fp32: `redux.sync` f32 exists only on sm_100a and sm_100f builds (22.1 cycles), not on the mandated sm_100, where twiddle plus redux u32 plus untwiddle costs 40 (T2). Value-only rounds need a winner election for ties (ballot on equality 27 plus a redux.min over lane ids 22, not ffs at 40), about 75 + 2 IPT, and prefer_smaller_index is not slot-major. N4 (truncated key plus index in one word, 20 key bits at N 4096) restores the 26-cycle packed round for fp32 with one tie pass of about 150, so fp32 costs bf16 plus 150.
- Against N1 (about 840 + 134 IPT for bf16): W2 wins up to k about 69 at N 256 and k about 77 at N 512, which leaves N1 only k 128 at N 256. Against W5 (about 1400 + 10 IPT at k 16, 2210 + 10 IPT at k 17 to 64): W2 wins at k 16 up to N 1024 and at k 32 up to N 512, ties W5 at N 512 k 64 (2470 against 2440), and loses from N 1024 k 32 (2950 against 2630) and from N 2048 k 16 (round 180). Against W3 at 4-bit digits (1028 + 104 IPT): W2 wins at k 16 up to N 4096 on the model (5860 against 14.3k), but W5 at 3800 takes that cell.

focus cells: 1. warp N 256 k 8 bf16 (DeepSeek V3, Kimi K2 shape), 2. warp N 512 k 16 bf16 and fp32 (Qwen3.5, Kimi K3 shape), 3. sub-warp LW 8 N 128 k 8 bf16 (4 rows per warp, gpt-oss-120b and Qwen3 routing), 4. warp N 512 k 32 bf16 (Nemotron 3 Ultra k 22), 5. warp N 1024 k 16 fp32 with N4 (Kimi K3 896 rounded up, PEER, NSA at 64K).
boundary cells: warp N 512 k 64 (expect W5 or N1 to win), warp N 2048 k 16 (IPT 64, list shift exceeds the redux latency, expect W5).

explore first: (1) reproduce T19's round inside the full primitive (prologue, k rounds, emission) at IPT 4, 8, 16, 32 as a slope over k, with the whole list consumed at the end (T19 pitfall: otherwise the compiler drops every shift but the head's and the round reads 25 cycles at any IPT). (2) per-lane list structure above IPT 8, since the shift is the only N-dependent term: a two-level list (sorted top 8 per lane plus an unsorted remainder refilled by a per-lane max when the top 8 drains) keeps the round at 26 for any IPT at the price of an occasional refill, against the measured 89 at IPT 32. (3) fp32 through N4, against value-only rounds with the redux.min election for prefer_smaller_index. (4) sub-warp via masked redux with 2, 4, 8 rows per warp. (5) two candidates per round (a second redux over the lanes whose head was not the winner, 22 more cycles for the second item) only if (1) confirms the 26-cycle round inside the primitive, the gain is then about 1.2x.

determinism and ordering: packed form inherent and sorted, prefer_smaller_index by inverting the index. Value-only fp32 form gives set membership for distinct values, ties need the election or N4.

main risk: the round is measured, the risk moved to the list. Above IPT 8 the round grows at 2.8 cycles per item (89 at IPT 32, 64 registers), so N 1024 and above belongs to W2 only with the two-level list of explore item 2, and the whole row plus the list in registers limits occupancy in the throughput regime (45 registers at IPT 8 in the chain kernel).

cheapest kill experiment: explore item 1 at IPT 8 and 32 (N 256 and 1024) with k 8 to 32 inside the full primitive. If the primitive's round at IPT 8 exceeds 40 cycles (T19 measured 26 in isolation), the emission or prologue is eating the gain and the k 32 cells at N 512 move to W5.
