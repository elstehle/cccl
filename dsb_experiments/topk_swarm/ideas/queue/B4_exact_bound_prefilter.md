# B4: Exact-bound prefilter, compaction and finish (block, N 8192 and above)
status: queued (wave 2, first block card to launch)
scope: block
origin: new, now a composition of three measured pieces: the thread-max bracket of N6 (T25, B1_n6 cards), the compaction path and scan emission of B3 (T29, T31, B3_tuned cards), and the W2 or radix finish over compacted candidates
hypothesis: the minimum over threads of the per-thread maximum is an exact lower bound on the k-th largest for every k at most 256 (256 items are at least that large) and costs about 150 cycles inside the barrier the histogram zeroing needs anyway (measured in N6). Items at or above it are a fraction 1 minus (1 over 257) to the power 1 over IPT of the tile on random input: 75 percent at IPT 4, 29 at 16, 16 at 32, 8.3 at 64, 4.2 at 128. B1's first histogram pass costs about 85 cycles per item slot (10.9k of the 16.4k at N 32768), a compare against the bound and a predicated store cost about 3 instructions per item. So from IPT 32 on B4 replaces the dominant pass by a sweep, compacts the 4 to 16 percent of survivors with B3's padded buffer and packed scan, and finishes with two cheap passes over the candidates (B1 or N6 passes at IPT 5, or W2 when at most 32 remain, N9). k above 256 keeps the top m per thread for the bound (m IPT compares). Index-only output and prefer_smaller_index come from the scan emission in index order at zero cost (B3 measured 0.997x to 1.003x).
cells it should win: block N 8192 to 32768, k 64 to 2048, both types, random and sorted input (striped arrangement makes sorted input benign)

what it must beat (measured 2026-10-25, B200, cycles, bf16 then fp32): B1_idx N 32768 k 64 16864 and 18804, k 256 18686 and 21788, k 1024 24574 and 28543. B3_tuned N 32768 k 256 19167 and 20071, k 2048 19253 and 20804, N 16384 k 64 10990 and 10469, k 1024 11009 and 11328, N 8192 k 64 6275 and 6083. N6 (B1_n6b) N 32768 fp32 k 16 14090, k 64 18073, k 256 21656. B2_twolevel N 32768 bf16 k 16 4774 (fp32 7446), k 64 22947, N 16384 bf16 k 16 3252, k 64 13181, N 8192 bf16 k 16 2643, k 64 13551. To matter B4 has to be 1.5x faster than the best of these per cell: at most 11k at N 32768 k 64, 12.5k at k 256, 7k at N 16384 k 64, 4k at N 8192 k 64. The k 16 cells belong to B2_twolevel (4774) and are out of reach.

cost model (measured constants: bracket about 150 (N6 cards), B3 compaction sweep about 12 instructions per item slot plus a 300-cycle packed scan plus the padded candidate loads, B1 later passes about 5 instructions per item slot plus 410 per pass, histogram pass 85 per item slot, barrier 28):
- Sweep: per item one compare, one count increment, one predicated store of (key, index) to the padded buffer, about 3 to 5 instructions, IPT times that. At IPT 128 about 600 to 800 cycles against 10.9k for the histogram pass.
- Compaction bookkeeping: one packed block scan (300), one barrier, candidate loads (padded, T31) about 2 per candidate per thread.
- Finish: C = f N candidates, two radix or fixed-point passes at IPT_c = C over 256: 2 times (85 IPT_c + 410). N 32768 (C about 1380, IPT_c 5.4) about 1740. N 8192 (C 1310) about 1740. Or W2 by warp 0 when C is at most 32 (N9).
- Emission: packed scan, predicated stores, about 300 plus the selected stores.
- Totals on the model: N 32768 about 150 + 700 + 300 + 300 + 1740 + 300 = 3500, N 16384 about 3000, N 8192 about 2700. B3's measured compaction path (20.9k at N 32768 k 256 with an 11k first pass and 4600 candidates) says the real bookkeeping is 2 to 3 times the model, so expect 6k to 9k at N 32768, still 2x to 3x under B1_idx and B3 at k 64 to 256, and about 2700 to 4000 at N 8192 against B1 6591 at k 64.
- Tighter bound for IPT at most 16 (29 percent candidates at IPT 16 is too many): the k-th largest of the 256 thread maxima by W2 on one warp (100 + 26 k, 520 at k 16, 1770 at k 64) brings the candidates to about k, so N 4096 k 64 is about 150 + 1770 + 200 + 300 + 900 + 300 = 3600 against N6 3774 and B1_idx 4587 (fp32), a tie at best. B4 starts at N 8192.

focus cells: 1. block N 32768 k 256 fp32, 2. block N 32768 k 64 bf16, 3. block N 16384 k 64 bf16, 4. block N 8192 k 64 fp32, 5. block N 32768 k 1024 fp32 (top-4 per thread bound, DSA V4 Pro chunk).
