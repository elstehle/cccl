# N6: Equi-width bucket select over the value bracket (block part measured in wave 1, warp part and the block follow-ups for wave 2)
status: queued (wave 2: warp scope with W3 and N1 in one optimizer, block follow-ups with opt-B1's successor)
scope: block, warp
origin: new here, the 256-bin uniform histogram of the TRT-LLM GVR kernel inside the primitive. Block part built and measured by opt-B1 and fix-B1 (cards B1_n6_b200_20261025_2 and B1_n6b, catalogue T24 and T25).
hypothesis: a bracket [lo, hi] with hi the max and lo the minimum over threads of the per-thread maximum (exact for k at most Threads) turns one 256-bin pass into the discriminating pass that two radix passes give. Measured at block scope: the pivot bin after one pass holds 1.1 to 7.3 items on average and at most 16 for every k at most 256 at N 1024 to 32768 on the harness random input (host simulation confirmed on the GPU), and the per-item bin function costs 4 instructions over the radix digit. The win against B1_idx is real where a pass is saved and lost where the bracket and the heavier pass are not paid back: fp32 N 4096 k 16 to 256 ahead by 24, 18 and 11 percent, N 1024 k 16 ahead by 5 to 7 percent, bf16 k 64 and above at N 1024 behind by 9 to 18 percent, and throughput behind by 5 to 32 percent everywhere through registers (46 against 32 at IPT 4, 255 with 7.3 KB of spill at IPT 128). At warp scope the same bracket with 32 bins by 5 ballots per item replaces W3's 4 to 8 digit passes by one, and a lo from the per-lane top m (m = ceil(k over 32)) extends the exact bound to k 1024.
cells it should win: warp N 512 to 4096 with k 64 to 1024 (both types, where W5 has no cheap bound and W2's list is deep). Block: N 4096 fp32 at k 16 to 256 already, N 1024 to 4096 k 16 with the W2 finish (N9), and N 32768 once the register problem is solved.

what it must beat (B200, 2026-10-25). Warp: W2_packed v1 and v3 at N 512 k 64 2704 and 4599, k 256 12362 and 12614, N 1024 k 64 6628 and 5917, N 2048 k 64 9237 and 8097, k 256 40417 and 42931, W5 predicted 2740 at N 512 k 64 and 3220 at N 2048 k 64, N7 predicted 2400 to 2600 at k 64 and 8300 at N 2048 k 256, W3 at 4-bit digits predicted 2700 at N 512 and 7700 at N 2048 (bf16, double for fp32). Block: B1_idx (cells in the B4 card), B2 at k 16 (1380 at N 1024, 2057 at N 4096), and N6's own measured numbers (B1_n6b: fp32 N 1024 k 16 1664, N 4096 k 16 2801, k 64 3774, k 256 4467, bf16 N 1024 k 64 2039, N 32768 fp32 k 16 14090, bf16 k 16 19177).

cost model, warp (measured: dependent ballot 27 and 6 per further ballot T17, redux 22, shuffle stage about 44 at IPT 1, W2 round 26 at list depth at most 8 T38):
- Bracket: per-lane top m (m IPT compares, m 1 for k at most 32, 2 for k 64, 8 for k 256), two redux (44), bin scale once.
- One pass: 5 ballots per item (27 + 30 IPT), one bucket count per lane (2 IPT + 10), a 5-step scan (about 150), crossing and broadcast (50). Emission of the items above the pivot bin by ballot prefix in slot-major order (27 + 6 IPT + 50). Finish on the pivot bin: W2 by the warp on at most 32 candidates (100 + 26 k_rem), or the W3 key-bit passes when the bin is a bf16 tie class or overflows.
- Totals bf16 and fp32 alike: N 512 k 64 about 2000 (against W2 2704, W5 2740), N 1024 k 128 about 2750 (W2 above 10k, W3 4360 and 8700), N 2048 k 256 about 4700 (W2 40417, W3 7700 and 15.4k, N7 8300), N 2048 k 64 about 3900 (W5 3220 wins, the 5 ballots per item cost more than W5's compare at IPT 64), N 4096 k 1024 about 8500 (W1_reference 397814 and 337437).
- Block follow-ups: (a) registers at IPT 128 (something holds 128 words per thread across the passes with the pin of T24 in place, candidates are the twiddled key per item and the second image copy), a two-half item loop bounds either. (b) the W2 finish on the pivot bin instead of a second pass (N9): saves about 400 to 500 cycles at N 1024 to 4096 and about 1700 at N 32768 on the model. (c) the representable-value exit for bf16 after the first pass. (d) the throughput gap is occupancy (6 against 8 blocks per SM at IPT 4, 3 against 5 at IPT 16), so (a) decides whether N6 can be a batched primitive.

focus cells (warp): 1. N 512 k 64 bf16, 2. N 1024 k 128 fp32, 3. N 2048 k 256 bf16, 4. N 2048 k 64 fp32 (against W5), 5. N 4096 k 1024 fp32. Block (follow-ups): N 1024 k 16 both types with the W2 finish, N 32768 k 64 fp32 with the register fix.
boundary cells: warp N 512 k 16 (W2 820 expected to win 2x), warp N 2048 k 16 (W5 1670 expected).

explore first: (1) warp: the bracket pass alone at IPT 16, 64 and 128 as a slope, against one 4-bit radix pass (W3) in the same session. (2) warp: the full primitive at N 512 k 64 and N 2048 k 256 bf16 against W2_packed v1 and the W3 fallback. (3) block: the register attribution at IPT 128 (ptxas -v of n6c against idx1, two-half item loop). (4) block: W2 finish on the pivot bin (N9) at N 1024 and 4096 k 16. (5) block: outlier handling, switch to key digits when the pivot bin holds more than a quarter of the candidates (bracket collapse on heavy tails, oracle pattern inf_denorm already passes).

determinism and ordering: block as B1 (ticket order, warp-redundant rank or the B3 scan for prefer_smaller_index). Warp: the W2 finish orders by (key, index), the emission is slot-major, so prefer_smaller_index is free. Signed zero shares an image, infinities clamp, NaN excluded by contract.

main risk: at warp scope 5 ballots per item is 30 cycles per item slot, above W5's compare-based filter, so N6 warp owns only the k range W5 cannot bound (k above 64) and loses it again to N7 where W2's two-level list keeps the round at 30 cycles (k 64 to 128). At block scope the register footprint has flipped between builds of the same algorithm (1.5 KB to 8.6 KB of spill at IPT 128) and throughput is behind B1_idx in every cell until it is fixed.

cheapest kill experiment: warp: explore item 2 at N 512 k 64 bf16 against W2_packed v1 (2704) and W5 in one session, N6 warp must be at least 1.3x under W2 (at most 2080) to matter. Block: explore item 4, the W2 finish must take at least 15 percent off N6b at N 1024 k 16.
