# N9: Packed W2 finish on the compacted pivot bin (for N6, B4 and the B1 early exit)
status: queued (wave 2, small, attach to the B4 optimizer or to N6's block follow-ups)
scope: block
origin: new, from two wave 1 measurements: the pivot bin after N6's first pass holds 1.1 to 7.3 items on average and at most 16 for every k at most 256 at N 1024 to 32768 (T25, `approaches/B1/sim_passes.md`, confirmed on the GPU in cards B1_n6 and B1_n6b), and the packed W2 round costs 26 cycles at list depth at most 8 (T38). The July FINISH knob of the AIR study (a warp bitonic finish for at most 64 candidates) measured plus 52 cycles on the common case and is superseded by this.
hypothesis: after the first bucket pass the selected-above items are final and the pivot bin holds a handful of candidates of which k_rem are wanted. N6 today runs a second full pass for them: a predicated sweep over all IPT item slots (about 5 instructions per slot), a scan (410) and three barriers, about 750 cycles at IPT 4 and about 1700 at IPT 128. Instead the pivot bin's members write their packed (key, index) word to shared memory by one ticket each (atomicAdd with return, 55 cycles, pipelined, a handful of them), one barrier, warp 0 loads at most 32 words (one per lane, 33 cycles) and runs k_rem packed W2 rounds at 26 cycles each, then emits the indices by position. About 150 + 26 k_rem, typically 250 to 400 against 750 to 1700. Exact: the tie class of the k-th word is ordered by index inside the packed word, so prefer_smaller_index is inherent and the bf16 tie-class exit of N6 is unnecessary on this path. When the bin exceeds 32 words warp 0 runs W2 at list depth 2 to 8 (still 26 per round up to 256 candidates) and above that the second pass stays.
cells it should win: every N6 cell where the second pass is replaced: block N 1024 to 4096 k 16 to 256 both types, N 32768 fp32 k 16 to 256. B4's finish over its compacted candidates when they are at most 256. The B1 early exit when the pivot bucket after the first radix pass is small (56 to 139 candidates on random input, T25, so only at k_rem at most about 20).

what it must beat (B200, 2026-10-25, cycles): N6 itself, B1_n6b bf16 N 1024 k 16 1685, k 64 2039, N 4096 k 16 2748, k 64 4043, fp32 N 1024 k 16 1664, N 4096 k 16 2801, k 64 3774, k 256 4467, N 32768 k 16 14090. The incumbents N6 then has to pass: B2_packed N 1024 k 16 1380 and N 4096 k 16 2057 (bf16), B2 at 128 threads 1182 and 1907, B1_idx N 1024 k 64 1878, N 4096 k 64 3838 and 4587 (bf16, fp32). To matter N9 must take at least 15 percent off N6b at N 1024 and 4096 k 16 (at most 1430 and 2340 bf16), which would bring N6 within 5 percent of B2 at k 16 and make it k-flat up to 256 where B2 is 5397 at k 64.

cost model (measured: W2 round 26 at list depth at most 8 (T38), atomicAdd with return 55 uncontended and one per cycle under contention (T9), barrier 28, LDS 33, N6 second pass about 5 instructions per item slot plus 410 scan plus barriers):
- Ticket and store by the pivot bin members: about 55 + 10 per member, pipelined, plus one barrier. Warp 0: one LDS per lane, the W2 prologue on a list of 1 (nothing), k_rem rounds at 26, index emission by position (STS per round, off the chain). About 150 + 26 k_rem.
- Saved: the second pass, 85 times the predicated fraction plus the sweep over IPT slots (about 5 IPT cycles at 2 warps per scheduler) plus 410 plus 2 barriers: about 650 at IPT 4, 900 at IPT 16, 1700 at IPT 128.
- Net: N 1024 k 16 bf16 1685 to about 1250 (k_rem about 4), N 4096 k 16 2748 to about 2150 (B2 2057), N 4096 k 64 4043 to about 3500 (B1_idx 3838), N 4096 k 256 fp32 4467 to about 3900 (B1_idx 5047), N 32768 fp32 k 16 14090 to about 12600 (B2_twolevel 7446 wins that cell regardless).
- Risk term: when k_rem is large (the pivot bin has up to 16 items and all are wanted) the finish is 150 + 416, still under the pass.

focus cells: 1. block N 1024 k 16 bf16 and fp32 (N6 with the W2 finish against N6b and B2 in one session), 2. block N 4096 k 16 bf16, 3. block N 4096 k 64 fp32, 4. block N 4096 k 256 fp32, 5. B4's compacted candidates at N 32768 k 64 once B4 exists.
boundary cells: block N 1024 k 256 bf16 (pivot bin can be a tie class of 18 words, W2 at depth 1 still fine, but the selected-above emission dominates), block N 32768 k 16 (B2_twolevel expected to keep the cell).

explore first: (1) N6b with the second pass replaced by the warp 0 W2 finish when the pivot bin count is at most 32, at N 1024 and 4096 k 16, both types, against N6b and B2_packed in one session. (2) the threshold between the W2 finish and the second pass (32, 128, 256 candidates) from the slope of the W2 finish over list depth. (3) the same finish in B1_idx after the first radix pass when the pivot bucket is at most 128 and k_rem at most 20. (4) B4's finish once B4's compaction exists.

determinism and ordering: the finish orders the pivot bin by (key, index), the selected-above items keep their emission order (ticket or scan), so prefer_smaller_index holds under the scan emission and the output is unsorted overall.

main risk: the ticket and the extra barrier are a fixed 150 cycles that the saved pass must exceed, which holds from IPT 4 on but leaves little at k_rem near 16. The occupancy problem of N6 (46 against 32 registers) is untouched by this card.

cheapest kill experiment: explore item 1. If the W2 finish does not take at least 15 percent off N6b at N 1024 k 16 (at most 1430 bf16), drop the card.
