status: queued (wave 2b, the chunk merge first, then the sorted-output crossover, no not_guaranteed cell expected)
scope: warp, block (the chunk merge has a block form)
origin: `reference/WARP_MERGE_SORT_RESULTS.md`, `reference/SUBWARP_SORT_RESULTS.md`, `cub/cub/warp/warp_merge_sort.cuh`, and the chunk-merge item inherited from the killed B6 card (`ideas/rejected/R17_B6_sort_when_k_large.md`)
hypothesis: per-lane sorted runs plus merge-path merges that keep only the top k. Wave 1 and wave 2 settled every not_guaranteed cell for W2, W5 and the bucket selects, so W4 has two uses left. First, the k-truncated multiway merge of M sorted chunk results (the DSA and sampling merge cells of `ideas/WORKLOADS.md` section 7), where the inputs arrive sorted from W2, W5 or a sort and the merge is O(k log2 M) work with no selection. Second, the sorted-output pass (phase 3b) at k at least 256 at warp scope, where the only sorted incumbents are W2's deep-list rounds and the W1 reference.
cells it should win: chunk merges of M 4 to 8 sorted runs of k 128 to 2048 (warp or block form), and warp N 2048 to 4096 with k at least N over 4 under the sorted or stable requirement

what it must beat (B200, 2026-10-25). Chunk merge, M 4 runs of k 2048 (8192 candidates, the DSA V3.2 merge shape): the unsorted selection over the candidates is B3_tuned at N 8192 k 2048, 4935 to 6477 cycles (fp32) and 6154 to 6328 (bf16), and the sorted alternatives are B6's full sort of the 8192 words, 32192 to 32513 (fp32) and 13505 to 13640 (bf16), or B3_tuned plus a post-sort of the k selected words, about 18.4k (fp32) and 11.9k (bf16) plus an unmeasured gather (T54, B6 report section 6). A merge of sorted runs that lands under 6.5k is the cheapest sorted route and under about 4k it also beats the unsorted selection. Sorted output at warp scope: W2 v1 at N 2048 k 1024 150107 (bf16) and 154812 (fp32), k 256 40417 and 42931, W1_reference 188007 and 131669 at k 1024, which a merge sort at about 30k on the July model beats by 4x to 5x. At k 64 and below the sorted incumbents are W5_v4 (N 1024 k 64 2936 and 4737, N 2048 k 64 4163 and 5975, sorted by its finish word) and W2_packed_v3 (N 1024 k 32 2481, N 512 k 64 2704), out of W4's reach. Under not_guaranteed the bar at k at least N over 4 is the warp bucket select of N6 (running, predicted 5k to 8.5k at N 2048 to 4096 k 1024) and W4 is predicted 4x to 6x behind it.

cost model (July placeholders: `WarpMergeSort` about 2000 + 470 IPT baseline, tuned static merge path 15 to 30 percent less, compiler sensitive. Measured constants: shuffle 4.9 per further shuffle in flight T17, T20 for the branch codegen on aarch64 hosts, T34 and T36 for unrolled networks and register arrays, T54 for the block sort passes):
- Per-lane sort of IPT items (network, under 500 compare-exchanges per unrolled loop per T34) + 5 merge levels per 32 lanes, each a static binary merge-path search plus a serial merge of IPT items. Truncation to k only idles lanes beyond k over IPT in a run unless the kept items are re-packed, so model the truncated cost as the untruncated merge sort until runs exceed k, then a compaction and half the items per lane per further level. N 2048 k 1024 fp32: about 2000 + 470 times 64 at the untruncated levels, above 30k.
- Chunk merge of M sorted runs of k, warp form: one merge-path split per lane into M runs (M binary searches of log2 k steps, 2 loads each) plus a serial merge of k over 32 items per lane, about M log2 k times 2 loads plus k over 32 steps, so about 400 + 20 k over 32 for M 4 and k 2048, under 2000. Block form at 256 threads: k over 256 items per thread after the partition, one block scan free emission (T29), the same order of cost with a shared-memory staging of the runs.

focus cells: 1. chunk merge of 4 sorted runs of 2048 fp32 as one block (against B3_tuned on 8192 items and B6's numbers), 2. chunk merge of 8 sorted runs of 1024 fp32 as one warp (DSA V4 Pro merge shape), 3. warp N 2048 k 1024 fp32 sorted output (against W2 v1 and N6 warp plus a post-sort), 4. warp N 4096 k 2048 bf16 sorted output.
boundary cells: warp N 512 k 256 (W2 12362 and N6 warp expected to win), warp N 2048 k 64 (W5 4163 expected).
