# N8: Block size as a free parameter (64 and 128 threads for B2 rounds, 512 threads for radix passes at large N)
status: done, settled (opt-N8, 2026-10-25, cards N8_b2packed_b200_20261025_1 to _3, N8_b1idx, N8_b3tuned, catalogue T43 to T46 and T50). B2: 128 threads for latency at N 1024 to 4096 and k up to 32 (1.07x to 1.20x over 256 threads, the k 64 row is node dependent and loses to B1 at 256 threads on every node), 64 threads never win latency (the round is 48 + 4 W + IPT at W warps, T43 and T46, the 64-thread form is 0.83x of 128 at N 1024 k 16), 64 threads win the one-wave throughput at N up to 2048 by 1.2x to 1.66x at the same threads per SM (T44, B1_idx shows the same flip at 1.2x to 1.6x), 256 threads from N 8192, 512 threads only for k 4 at N 8192, and the two-level list at 256 threads stays the N 32768 shape (a 16-warp round costs 175 to 203 cycles). Radix (B1_idx, B3_tuned, N6): 512 threads from N 8192 in latency and from N 16384 in throughput, 1.2x to 1.45x over 256 threads with 64 items per thread in 100 to 103 registers and no spill (T45), 1024 threads never. B1_idx at 512 threads is the large-N incumbent for the final evaluation (13530 bf16 and 11797 fp32 cycles at N 32768 k 16, 585 and 664 Gelem per s), B3_tuned at 512 threads keeps a latency-only edge over it at k 1024 (3 to 22 percent) plus free prefer_smaller_index, and its throughput edge is gone. One of three B200 nodes measures fully unrolled 128-thread kernels above about 64 KB 25 to 33 percent slower (T50), the verifier's node choice for such cells.
scope: block
origin: new, from opt-B2b's card B2_packed_b200_20261025_3 (128 threads: round 69.5 cycles at IPT 8 against 83.5 at 256 threads, N 1024 k 16 bf16 1182 against 1380, N 4096 k 16 1907 against 2057, k 64 6254 against 7290, crossover with B1 moved from k 22 to 28 at N 1024 and from 34 to 64 at N 4096), from T11 (barrier 20 cycles at 128 threads against 28 at 256) and from the IPT 128 register and spill problems of every block primitive at 256 threads (B1_reference, N6, B3).
hypothesis: a block round costs barrier plus one aggregated CREDUX per warp plus read back, so fewer warps per block make the round cheaper (69.5 at 4 warps, 83.5 at 8) while the per-thread list grows with the doubled IPT. The measured 128-thread kernel wins 1.08x to 1.17x in latency and 1.0x to 1.1x in throughput. A 64-thread block (2 warps, IPT 16 at N 1024) has a barrier of about 16 cycles and 2 CREDUX per round, so the round is predicted near 55 to 60 cycles with a 63-comparator sort16 prologue (about 130 cycles) and a list depth of min(k, 16) that stays pruned to 8 for k at most 8: about 130 + 16 times 58 = 1060 at N 1024 k 16 against 1182 (128 threads), 1380 (256) and 1485 for one warp (W2_packed_v3 at IPT 32). In the other direction, radix and bucket passes at N 16384 and 32768 run at 255 registers with spills at 256 threads (IPT 64 and 128), and 512 threads halve IPT (B1_idx is at 47 registers and 0 spill at IPT 16), so the pass sweeps shorten and the scan over 256 bins stays one bin per thread with two threads per bin or 512 bins at 9-bit digits (T30), at the price of a 512-thread barrier.
cells it should win: B2 at 64 or 128 threads: block N 1024 to 4096, k 8 to 32, both types (the B2 cells, 1.1x to 1.3x). Radix at 512 threads: B1_idx, B3_tuned and N6 at N 16384 to 32768 where the 256-thread kernels spill.

what it must beat (B200, 2026-10-25): B2_packed at 256 threads bf16 N 1024 k 16 1380, N 4096 k 16 2057, k 64 7290, fp32 1680 and 2299, and at 128 threads 1182, 1907, 6254, 1494, 2085. W2_packed_v3 at one warp N 1024 k 16 1485 and 1547. B1_idx N 32768 k 64 16864 and 18804 (255 registers, 0 spill), N6 (B1_n6b) N 32768 fp32 k 64 18073 (255 registers, 7.3 KB spill), B3_tuned N 32768 k 256 19167 and 20071 (255, 64 to 696 bytes spill). To matter: B2 at 64 threads at least 1.15x under the 128-thread kernel at N 1024 k 16, radix at 512 threads at least 1.15x under the 256-thread kernel at N 32768 k 64.

cost model (measured: block round 83.5 at 8 warps, 69.5 at 4 warps, barrier 28 and 20 (T11), CREDUX 22 (T1), LDS 33, W2 round 26 at 1 warp (T38), B1 histogram pass about 85 per item slot, scan 410 per pass at 256 threads):
- B2 round at W warps: about barrier(W) + 22 + 33 + head select, with barrier about 16, 20, 28 at 2, 4, 8 warps, so about 58, 70, 84. Prologue: the per-thread sort of IPT = N over (32 W) items, sort16 63 comparators at 64 threads and N 1024, sort32 191 at N 2048.
- N 1024 k 16 at 64 threads about 130 + 16 times 58 = 1060, k 32 about 2000 (B2 at 256 threads 2740 for k 32, B1_idx 1878 at k 64 bf16). N 2048 k 16 at 64 threads: sort32 prologue about 540 plus 16 times 58, about 1470 against 128 threads about 1600 and 256 threads about 1700.
- Radix at 512 threads, N 32768 (IPT 64): histogram pass sweeps 64 slots per thread instead of 128, the MIO-bound atomic count is unchanged (32768 per pass), the predicated later passes halve, the scan over 256 bins by 512 threads needs a two-thread-per-bin fold, the barrier costs more. Predicted 5 to 15 percent in latency from the shorter dependent sweeps and the removed spills, and a throughput gain where the 256-thread kernel sits at 1 block per SM with spills (bf16 N6 at N 32768: 139 to 364 Gelem per s against B1_idx 459).

focus cells: 1. B2 at 64 threads, N 1024 k 16 bf16 (against 128 and 256 threads and W2 at one warp, one session), 2. B2 at 64 threads, N 2048 k 16 fp32, 3. B1_idx at 512 threads, N 32768 k 64 fp32 against 256 threads, 4. N6 at 512 threads, N 32768 k 64 fp32 (the spill cell), 5. B3_tuned at 512 threads, N 32768 k 256 bf16.
boundary cells: B2 at 64 threads N 4096 k 16 (IPT 64, the sort64 prologue of 543 comparators, T34, expected to lose to 128 threads), B1_idx at 512 threads N 4096 (expected to lose, IPT 8 has no spills to remove).

explore first: (1) B2_packed at 64 threads with the full sort16 prologue and the B2_twolevel head for IPT 32, N 1024 and 2048, k 8 to 32, both types, against 128 and 256 threads in one session. (2) B1_idx at 512 threads at N 16384 and 32768, registers and spills first (ptxas -v), then latency and throughput. (3) N6 and B3_tuned at 512 threads at N 32768. (4) the device agent question: with several small blocks per SM the per-scheduler CREDUX budget (T33, about 5.5 cycles each) and the shared barrier resources decide the batched throughput, measure 64-thread B2 at one wave.

determinism and ordering: unchanged per approach (B2 inherent, radix as B1 or B3).

main risk: a 64-thread B2 holds IPT 16 to 32 items per thread with a 32 to 48 register list, and its throughput depends on how many 2-warp blocks the SM runs at once, which the harness's one-wave measurement captures only partly. 512-thread radix passes may lose their gain to the barrier and the two-thread scan fold.

cheapest kill experiment: explore item 1 at N 1024 k 16 bf16. If 64 threads are not at least 1.15x under 128 threads, B2's block size stays 128 and only the 512-thread radix item remains.
