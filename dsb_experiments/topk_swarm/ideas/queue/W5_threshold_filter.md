# W5: Threshold filter with an exact lane bound (warp, k 16 to 64 at N 1024 and above)
status: queued (wave 2, first warp card to launch, same optimizer as N7 since they compete for the same cells)
scope: warp (block scope is B4)
origin: `reference/GVR_V2_ALGORITHM_REPORT.md` (TRT-LLM self-sampling GVR), new at warp scope
hypothesis: a warp knows 32 lane maxima after IPT compares. Their k-th largest (k at most 32) is an exact lower bound on the k-th largest of the row, so there is no underflow and no retry. One compare per item keeps candidates at or above it, a ballot prefix compacts them to one per lane, and a packed W2 finish on at most 32 candidates (list depth 1, 26 cycles per round) is exact. For k 33 to 64 keep the top 2 per lane (64 values). The filter costs 2 compares and one sixth of a ballot per item, which is below W2's per-lane prologue (a sort of IPT items) and far below any ballot histogram, and it is compare-based, so fp32 costs what bf16 costs. W2's measured cost grows with the list depth above 8 and with its fp32 tie path (W2_packed_v3: N 2048 k 16 bf16 2377 but fp32 4325, k 64 9237 and 8097), which is the room W5 takes.
cells it should win: warp N 1024 to 4096 with k 32 to 64 (both types), N 2048 and above with k 16, fp32 at N 1024 and above from k 16 (NSA at 64K to 128K, EAGLE-2 rerank, PEER sub-keys), random input

what it must beat (W2_packed_v3 card, B200, 2026-10-25, cycles bf16 then fp32): N 512 k 32 1468 and 2227, k 64 2704 and 4599. N 1024 k 16 1485 and 1547, k 32 2481 and 3059, k 64 6628 and 5917. N 2048 k 16 2377 and 4325, k 32 3970 and 6449, k 64 9237 and 8097. N 4096 not measured by W2 (its k 256 and 1024 units compile for 20 minutes), W1_reference there is 155785 and 130474 at k 16. To matter W5 has to be 1.3x faster than W2 in a cell: at most 1900 at N 1024 k 32 bf16, 3050 at N 2048 k 32, 3300 at N 2048 k 16 fp32, 5100 at N 1024 k 64 bf16. N7 (two-level W2 list) predicts about 2400 at N 1024 k 64 and 2600 at N 2048 k 64, so W5 must also stay within 10 percent of N7 there or win on fp32.

cost model (measured 2026-10-25: W2 round 26 at list depth at most 8 inside the primitive (T38, W2 cards), packed sort32 574 bf16 and min max pairs sort32 531 fp32 (W1_eager_same, W1_minmax_pairs at N 32), sort64 761 and 720, dependent ballot 27 and 6 per further ballot (T17), popc 23):
- Lane maxima: IPT compares (about 2 IPT). Bound for k at most 32: W2 on the 32 maxima, 100 + 26 k (520 at k 16, 930 at k 32), or a sort32 at 531 to 574 and one shuffle. Top-2 bound for k 33 to 64: 2 IPT compares plus W2 on 64 words at list depth 2, 100 + 26 k (1760 at k 64), or sort64 at 720 to 761.
- Filter and compaction: IPT compares, IPT ballots pipelined (21 + 6 IPT), popc prefix and one predicated register move, about 60 + 8 IPT. Index carried in a second register or inside the bf16 word.
- Finish: W2 on the compacted candidates at list depth 1 or 2, 100 + 26 k.
- Totals: k at most 16 about 740 + 8 IPT + 26 k. N 1024 k 16: 1410 against W2 1485 and 1547, a tie. N 2048 k 16: 1670 against 2377 (1.42x) and 4325 (2.6x). N 4096 k 16: 2180. k 17 to 64 with the sort64 bound about 920 + 10 IPT + 26 k. N 512 k 32: 1240 against 1468 (1.18x). N 1024 k 32: 1570 against 2481 (1.58x) and 3059 (1.95x). N 2048 k 32: 1890 against 3970 (2.1x) and 6449 (3.4x). N 512 k 64: 2740 against 2704 and 4599, a bf16 tie and a 1.7x fp32 win. N 1024 k 64: 2900 against 6628 and 5917 (2.0x to 2.3x). N 2048 k 64: 3220 against 9237 and 8097 (2.5x to 2.9x).
- Candidate count on random input: N times (1 minus (1 minus k over 32) to the power 1 over IPT) for the lane-max bound, about k plus a few (N 512 k 16 gives 22, N 2048 k 16 gives 23). The top-2 bound at k 64 admits about 70 at IPT 32 and more at IPT 64, so the finish may need 3 words per lane there.
- Overflow (sorted blocked input, bf16 tie floods): more candidates than the finish holds, fall back to the warp bucket select (N6 warp or W3). Striped arrangement makes sorted input benign.

focus cells: 1. warp N 2048 k 16 fp32 (NSA at 128K), 2. warp N 1024 k 32 bf16 and fp32, 3. warp N 2048 k 64 bf16 (EAGLE-2 rerank is N about 600 k 60, run N 1024 k 64 as well), 4. warp N 1024 k 64 fp32, 5. warp N 4096 k 16 fp32.
boundary cells: warp N 512 k 16 (W2 820 expected to win), warp N 1024 k 128 (bound needs top-4 per lane, N6 warp or N7 expected).

explore first: (1) host simulation of the candidate count of both bounds on the harness generator (random fp32, bf16 with its ties, sorted, tie-heavy) at N 512 to 4096, no GPU. (2) one session: W5 against W2_packed_v3 at N 1024 k 32 and N 2048 k 16, both types. (3) the bound: W2 rounds against the packed sort32 and sort64. (4) the finish list depth for the top-2 bound at IPT 32 to 128. (5) against N7 at N 1024 and 2048 k 64 in one session once N7 exists.

determinism and ordering: exact by counting. Ties at the bound are inside the candidate set and the packed W2 finish orders by (key, index), so prefer_smaller_index is inherent and the output is sorted.

main risk: bf16 ties at the bound (the harness measures 1.4 to 54 ties at rank k) can push the candidate count past the finish capacity on logit-like data, then the fallback costs a bucket select. The bound itself costs a sort32 or W2 rounds before any item is filtered, so W5 never beats W2 below k 16 at N 1024.

cheapest kill experiment: explore item 1, minutes, then one GPU cell: W5 against W2_packed_v3 at N 1024 k 32 bf16. If W5 is not at least 1.3x faster (at most 1900 cycles) or the 99th percentile candidate count on bf16 exceeds the finish capacity, keep W5 for fp32 only.
