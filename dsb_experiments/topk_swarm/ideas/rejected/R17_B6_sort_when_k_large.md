# R17: B6, sort when k is large
status: rejected (2026-10-25, algo-researcher, on opt-B6's cards B6_radix_b200_20261025_1 and _2, B6_pairs_b200_20261025_1 and catalogue T54, T55)
scope: block
seed: the B6 approach card (sort and truncate at k above N over 4, the capacity routing and chunk merge cells)
idea: for k at least N over 4 a full sort of the tile plus truncation beats a selection, since the selection's epilogue grows with k.
reason: measured with the stock `cub::BlockRadixSort` on the packed 32-bit word (bf16 key high, index low, keys-only sort over the key bits, 4 passes) and the 64-bit word for fp32 (8 passes), against B3_tuned in one lock section: bf16 N 1024 k 512 4279 against 1879 (2.28x slower), N 2048 k 1024 5550 against 2550 (2.18x), N 4096 k 512 and 2048 2.10x and 2.35x, N 8192 k 2048 and 4096 2.13x and 2.22x, fp32 5.0x to 6.5x (N 2048 k 1024 13487 against 2509). Against B1_idx at N 2048 k 1024 1.89x and 4.71x. Throughput 0.35x to 0.44x and 0.14x to 0.18x of B3_tuned. The sort is k-flat and so is B3's scan emission (T29), so no k from N over 8 to N over 2 and no N in the block range changes the verdict. The card's cost model had half the pass count (the keys-only sort needs 4 passes for bf16, T55) and a quarter of the pass cost: a ranking pass is a 256-thread scan plus two sweeps plus a full exchange through shared memory plus six barriers, 740 + 82 x IPT cycles (T54), about 1390 at IPT 8, not the 350 to 400 of one block scan. A radix select moves no data before the emission, a radix sort moves every item through shared memory in every pass.
evidence: cards B6_radix_b200_20261025_1 (sort cost sweep and the kill cell, B200 umbriel-b200-020, nvcc 13.4.92), B6_radix_b200_20261025_2 (against B1_idx), B6_pairs_b200_20261025_1 (pairs sort against the packed word: 1.24x slower for bf16, a tie for fp32), T54 and T55 in `TECHNIQUES.md`, `approaches/B6/REPORT.md`.
what survives: three things. (1) The sorted-output premium for phase 3b (`approaches/B6/REPORT.md` section 6): B6's output is sorted at zero extra cost, so the premium over B3_tuned's unsorted selection is the whole gap, bf16 2400 cycles at N 1024 k 512, 3000 at N 2048 k 1024, 4640 at N 4096 k 2048, 7490 at N 8192 k 4096, fp32 7820, 10980, 17730, 27240. Against the alternative route (B3_tuned plus a post-sort of the k selected words with the same sort) the full sort wins for bf16 at k = N over 2 by 5 to 19 percent, the post-sort route wins at k at most N over 4 and for fp32 at every k (N 2048 k 1024: 13487 against 2509 + 9767 = 12276), and the post-sort route still needs an unmeasured gather of the selected keys. The third route, W4's merge of sorted chunk runs, is unmeasured and now sits in W4's card with the B6 numbers as its bar. (2) The keys-only sort with the index in the low bits (T55): the stable LSD sort orders ties by index, prefer_smaller_index costs nothing, 4 passes instead of 8 for bf16, 1.24x over the pairs sort. (3) The pass cost fit of T54 for any future sorted-output budget.
