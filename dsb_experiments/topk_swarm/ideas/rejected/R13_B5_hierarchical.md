# R13: B5, hierarchical warp then block composition
status: rejected (2026-10-25, algo-researcher, on opt-W2's cards B5_hier_b200_20261025_1 and _2)
scope: block
seed: the B5 approach card, revived once the packed W2 round measured 26 cycles against 74 for the block atomic round
idea: each of the 8 warps runs packed W2 on its share, one shared-memory exchange, warp 0 runs packed W2 over the 8 k candidates: two warp phases of about 26 k cycles against one block phase of about 83 k.
reason: measured against B2_packed in one lock section: N 1024 k 16 bf16 1194 against 1380 cycles (1.16x, the card's 1.15x threshold by one percent) with 361 against 494 Gelem per s (0.73x). Everywhere else B2 wins or ties: N 1024 k 4 378 against 380, k 32 1.14x latency at 0.64x throughput, N 4096 B2 1.1x to 1.4x faster in latency and 1.3x to 1.7x in throughput, N 16384 B2 faster except k 4. The measured model is B5 about 70 k + 250 against B2 about 85 k + 40 at N 1024: the round gap is 15 cycles per k (the merge list of 8 k words over 32 lanes grows with k) and B5's two prologues plus the exchange cost 200 more than B2's. In phase 2 one warp works while seven wait, so the one barrier per call buys no throughput, and warp 0's merge list costs registers (28 to 48 against 21, 2 against 3 blocks per SM at N 16384).
evidence: cards B5_hier_b200_20261025_1 (against B1_reference: 4.7x at k 4, 1.58x at k 16, 0.82x at k 32, N 1024 bf16) and B5_hier_b200_20261025_2 (against B2_packed), B200 umbriel-b200-047 and -091, nvcc 13.4.92, canaries flat, R squared 1.0000.
what survives: nothing at block scope for the focus set. The fp32 next-word check at block scope (each warp emits k + 1 words) would save about 250 cycles and not change the verdict. A latency-only consumer that needs one barrier per call at N 1024 k 8 to 32 could still prefer B5 by 1.10x to 1.16x, which is a consumer question and not a primitive ranking.
