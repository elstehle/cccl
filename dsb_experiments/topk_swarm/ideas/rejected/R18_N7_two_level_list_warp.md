# R18: N7, two-level per-lane list for the packed W2 rounds at warp scope
status: rejected (2026-10-25, algo-researcher, on opt-W5's card N7_twolevel_b200_20261025_1 and catalogue T48, T49)
scope: warp, subwarp
seed: the N7 idea card (keep a sorted head of 8 words per lane and an unsorted remainder, refill rarely, so the W2 round stays at 26 cycles for k above the pruned list depth)
idea: W2's round is 26 cycles only while k is at most the pruned list depth 8 (T38, T41), 50 at depth 16, 89 at 32, 147 at 64. A two-level list with a refill check every H rounds by one ballot under a warp-uniform branch was modeled at about 30 cycles per round for any k, 2370 at N 1024 k 64 against W2's 6628 and W5's predicted 2900.
reason: three builds, each explaining one cost. (1) With the block loop unrolled the cold refill network was replicated per block (3737 VIMNMX against 382, 11368 instructions in the latency kernel) and the instruction fetch cost more than the deep list it replaced: N 1024 k 64 9903 against W2 7308 (T48). (2) With the loop rolled and blocks of 4 rounds the lookahead test ("fewer than 8 valid head words") fires after a single win, so the refill ran in nearly every block and the round scaled with the tile: 70, 106, 190 cycles at IPT 16, 32, 64 (T49). (3) With blocks of 2 (the test needs five wins) the refill is rare and the round is 45 to 74 cycles, of which about 18 are the block control: a vote, two taken branches and runtime round indices. Final numbers against W2_packed_v3 in one lock section: bf16 N 1024 k 64 4623 against 7308 (1.58x, threshold 2x and at most 3300), k 128 8382 against 11872, N 2048 k 64 5371 against 10071 (1.88x), k 128 9451 against 21324 (2.26x), fp32 N 2048 k 32 2843 against 7353 (2.59x), losses at IPT 16 (0.72x to 0.79x at N 512) and bf16 N 1024 k 16 (0.92x). Against W5 in the same session N7 is 36 percent behind at bf16 N 1024 k 64 (4623 against W5_v2 3397, W5_v4 2936) and behind at every bf16 cell from k 32. The structure cannot reach its model on nvcc 13.4: the round needs per-block control that W2's unrolled rounds do not have, and a prefilter that bounds the list depth (W5) removes the problem instead of managing it.
evidence: card N7_twolevel_b200_20261025_1 (B200 umbriel-b200-028, nvcc 13.4.92, batches n7a, n7b, n7c under state/results/opt-W5/), T48 and T49 in `TECHNIQUES.md`, `approaches/W5/REPORT.md`.
what survives: N7 beats W5 at fp32 N 1024 k 32 (2227 against W5_v2's 2849) and ties at fp32 N 2048 k 16 (2267 against 2293 for W5_v4) across sessions, because the N4 word keeps W2's cheap route and N7 has no compaction phase, and its throughput is 1.26x to 2.64x over W2 at k 32 to 128 where the block control overlaps other warps. Both are footnotes for the final evaluation, not a code path: W5 covers the band. The two measured rules (keep a cold path in one copy by rolling the loop around it, size a refilled head as four blocks of rounds) go to the knowledge base.
