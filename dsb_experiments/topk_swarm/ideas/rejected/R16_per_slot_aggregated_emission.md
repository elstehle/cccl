# R16: Index emission by ballot per tile slot with a per-warp atomic base
status: rejected (2026-10-25, algo-researcher, on opt-B1's card B1_idxw_b200_20261025_1 and catalogue T22)
scope: block
seed: the original N6 and B4 idea cards ("index-only emission by ballot prefix, per-warp atomic base") and the knowledge base 4.5 emission discussion
idea: in the index-only epilogue replace one shared atomic ticket per selected item by one ballot per tile slot and warp, one atomic with return by lane 0, a shuffle broadcast of the base and a popc rank, to cut the atomics by the warp width.
reason: the per-slot chain (ballot 27, popc 23, atomic with return 55, shuffle 30, popc and store) is about 150 cycles and the `if (ballot != 0)` guard serializes the slots, so the epilogue costs about IPT times 150: 2317 against 1767 cycles at bf16 N 1024 k 16 (IPT 4), 59063 against 16461 at N 32768 (IPT 128, 3.6x slower), 24 to 73 percent slower across the focus grid in latency and 9 to 66 percent in throughput. Per-item tickets issue one independent atomic per selected item and the hardware overlaps them (T9, T10). B3's packed block scan emission (T29) is the better replacement where the k dependence of the tickets matters: one scan of about 300 cycles, one predicated store per selected item, k-flat, and positions in index order, which makes prefer_smaller_index free.
evidence: card B1_idxw_b200_20261025_1 (B200 umbriel-b200-068, nvcc 13.4.92, verified), SASS 1008 against 656 instructions at bf16 N 1024 with a BSSY to BSYNC region per slot, T21, T22 and T29 in `TECHNIQUES.md`.
what survives: the two measured emissions: per-item tickets (T21) for small k and the packed block scan (T29) for k at least about 64, both adopted in the B4, N6 and N9 cards. At warp scope with one slot per lane the ballot prefix is a single chain and remains the right form (W3, N1, N6 warp, W5 compaction).
