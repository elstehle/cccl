# R15: Speculative post, taking the redux off the block round's dependent chain
status: rejected (2026-10-25, algo-researcher, on opt-B2b's cards B2_spec_b200_20261025_1 and B2_spec3_b200_20261025_1 and catalogue T26, T27, T33)
scope: block
seed: the B2 card's open question after the first round measurement (a 22-cycle CREDUX sits on every round's chain once ptxas aggregates the atomic)
idea: post from the lanes that hold the warp's stale maximum and second maximum, refreshed by redux whose inputs do not depend on the round maximum, so the redux runs in the shadow of the barrier and the round is barrier plus atomic plus read back. Variants: per-lane addresses to defeat the aggregation (spec), a store into a per-warp word reduced after the barrier (spec3), a predicated red per warp (spec2, spec4).
reason: three structural facts, all measured. (1) More than one posting lane per warp means per-lane addresses, and the un-aggregated same-address atomic serializes at 32 to 59 cycles per warp instruction (T10), so spec lost 1.08x to 2.46x (median 1.48x) against B2_packed. (2) Nothing a warp issues before arriving at `__syncthreads` is in that barrier's shadow, the barrier completes when the last warp arrives, so every refresh redux cost its issue slots and, when consumed before the arrival, its latency (T26). (3) ptxas moves CREDUX across BAR.SYNC in both directions regardless of source order and materializes the uniform result into a vector register right after it, so in most rounds a redux landed after the barrier and on the chain anyway: spec3 measured 137 cycles per round at IPT 4 against 83.5 (whole call 2200 against 1380 at N 1024 k 16 bf16, 0.63x) with no atomics and fewer barriers per call. The predicated red form (spec2, spec4) became a divergent branch with the UR to R moves before it. The source has no tool to pin the issue point of a redux relative to the barrier on nvcc 13.4.
evidence: cards B2_spec_b200_20261025_1 (round 138 against 83.4 at IPT 4, 36 shapes) and B2_spec3_b200_20261025_1 (round 137, registers 28 to 34 against 21, occupancy 6 against 8), SASS addresses of the CREDUX placements in the B2b report, B200 umbriel-b200-091 and -047, nvcc 13.4.92.
what survives: the block round's floor at 256 threads is the aggregated post plus barrier plus read back, about 83 cycles at IPT 4. The remaining levers are structural: fewer warps per block (measured 69.5 at 128 threads, card B2_packed_b200_20261025_3, idea card N8) and fewer rounds, and a true split barrier (named `bar.arrive` and `bar.sync`, or an mbarrier) if anything is ever to overlap the barrier, which no card has tried.
