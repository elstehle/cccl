Reading notes. (3) A runner-up marked `(spills)` has register spills or local memory at that cell, so its margin is against a degraded form (B2_packed above 32 items per thread is the known case). (4) When a table's cells were measured on more than one node the `node` column says which, candidates of one cell always share a node and a session.
